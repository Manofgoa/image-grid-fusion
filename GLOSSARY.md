# Glossary — Image Grid Fusion

Words used in the code, the documentation, the workfiles and the conversation, with one meaning
each.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Grid (*grille*) | The whole composition, made of **cells** laid out by a **layout** |
| Cell (*cellule*) | One slot of the grid |
| Image | The content of a cell, **whatever its source** — still image, video, animated GIF, preview of a text / PDF or other file. "Image" never means "still image only" |
| Layout (*disposition*) | The arrangement of cells chosen in the layout strip |
| Advanced layout (*disposition avancée*) | A layout offered under the layout strip's **More** group, hidden until the group is expanded (`GridLayout.IsAdvanced`); the others are the **basic** layouts |
| Separator (*séparateur*) | A stretch of boundary between cells, dragged to resize them: it moves every cell on both of its sides, and no other |
| Arm (*bras*) | One of the four separators of the Grid's cross while both of its lines are straight |
| Selected cell | The cell the effects toolbar acts on |
| Effect (*effet*), also *cell effect* | A transformation of a cell + image pair, turned on or off from the effects toolbar: Background, Crop, Cutout, Upscale, Zoom, Animations, Rotate, Flip, Frames, Colors, Blur, Volume. Turned off, it keeps its settings |
| Background (*fond*) | The cell effect painting the fill behind an image — the automatic band color or a chosen one, at an opacity, solid or a gradient (the shared fill), the image's edges extended over the bands in its extending fills; on by default, off leaves the cell transparent; off in every cell while the Grid background is on |
| Shared fill (*remplissage partagé*) | The fill the cell's Background and the Grid background both draw, one painter: **Solid**, **Linear** — from the first color to a second one along an angle — or **Radial**, from the first color at the center to the second at the corners; the second color **Darker**, **Black**, **White** or **Chosen**, at its own opacity, or **Auto** — the cell's automatic color in a cell's Background, **Auto, last cell** in the Grid background; a cell's first color from its **From**: **Auto**, **Black**, **White** or **Chosen** |
| Edge extension (*extension des bords*) | The Background's fills other than Color — Corner pixel, Miter, Background corners: the outermost rows and columns of pixels of the image as drawn in the cell stretched out to the cell's edges, the corners filled with the image's corner pixel, a mitered joint (two flat colors split by the diagonal, a half-opaque line in the corner pixel's color over it) or the background color; **Edges** sets their opacity over the background color, **Blend** fades them into the background color with the distance from the image, **Soften** blurs them more with it |
| Crop (*recadrage*) | The cell effect keeping a part of the image — the **kept part** (*partie gardée*), set by four bars in fractions of the image as loaded, so it follows a turn or a flip — which then stands for the whole image: fitted by the fitting rule, its automatic background computed on it, the canvas sized on it; an aspect ratio may be kept (Free, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16). 10 % in from each edge when turned on; its **100 %** button brings the kept part back to the whole image, the ratio freed; its **Keep** button makes the kept part the cell's image, a still of its own named after its file, the other effects kept |
| Crop edit view (*vue d'édition du recadrage*) | What the selected cell shows while the Crop tab is selected and the crop on: the whole image fitted whole, the part cut off dimmed, the bars across it, the kept part's corners moving two bars at once, a drag inside the kept part moving it, the wheel scaling it; preview only |
| Cutout (*détourage*) | The cell effect keeping a part of the image, the rest made transparent: the main subject a model finds (**Subject**), the object under the points clicked (**Pick** — Shift + click adds a part, Ctrl + click leaves one out), or everything the **plain background** encloses (**Plain** — the edges' color, flooded from the edges within a **Tolerance**, no engine needed); the parts a subject or a pick surrounds kept (**Fill holes**), an outline's gaps closed before either fill (**Gaps**); its edge pushed out or eaten in (**Grow**), faded (**Soften**), or the whole inverted (**Invert**); the part left out transparent, or kept with treatments that combine — see-through, under a color, blurred, pixelated — each its own slider; a pick's **reach** (Auto, Part, Object, Whole) from a part of the object to all of it. While Pick is armed the selected cell shows the whole image see-through, the object under the cursor outlined before the click. Computed in the background on the image as loaded, the cell drawn uncut with a *Cutting out…* badge meanwhile; still images only |
| Cutout engine (*moteur de détourage*) | IS-Net (the subject) and MobileSAM (a click): never shipped, installed from the Component updates window into `cutout-engine\` next to the exe, running on the OCR engine's ONNX Runtime |
| Upscale (*agrandissement*) | The cell effect drawing the image from an enlargement of it by a model, Real-ESRGAN — **Quality** or **Fast**, **×2** or **×4**, a factor past 8192 px on the longer side refused — computed in the **Upscale window** only: the options choose, the **Upscale** button or the checkbox computes; a still enlarged whole, an animated GIF frame by frame into the temp folder; again in each session, an image whose Upscale is on without its enlargement marked *Not upscaled* and enlarged before a Copy or a Save of the grid; the PNG's native size grows with it; with the Cutout on, the enlarged image is cut out. **Save upscaled image…**, in the cell's menu once computed, writes the enlargement alone to a PNG — an animated GIF for a GIF —, the cell untouched |
| Upscale window (*fenêtre Upscale*) | The modal window computing an enlargement: the size, the PNG's weight and the duration it will take — the last two estimated — then its progress, the time left and Cancel, the rest of the app waiting |
| Upscale engine (*moteur d'agrandissement*) | Real-ESRGAN ×2 and ×4 (Quality) and Real-ESRGAN compact (Fast): two components, never shipped, installed from the Component updates window into `upscale-engine\` next to the exe, running on the OCR engine's ONNX Runtime |
| Resizable zone (*zone dimensionnable*) | A rectangle set by an effect's four green bars, in fractions of its bounds — the crop's kept part in the image, the blur's sharp rectangle in the cell, its bars solid along it and dashed beyond it: while they show, the wheel over the selected cell grows or shrinks it, 5 % a notch, its ratio kept, around its center or the point under the cursor with Ctrl; with Alt, the wheel zooms the image instead, as without the bars |
| Global effect (*effet global*) | A transformation of the whole grid, turned on or off from the Global toolbar: Soundtrack, Fade, Borders, Grid background, Cascade, Layers |
| Global setting (*réglage global*) | A value of the whole grid always in force, set from a Global toolbar tab without activation checkbox: the output format |
| Global toolbar (*barre Global*) | The mirror of the effects toolbar at the bottom of the window: the "Global" label, the Format tab, one tab per global effect and the Reset button, standing on the global options toolbar. Formerly the *global effects toolbar* |
| Global options toolbar | The always-visible row just above the bottom bar, below the Global toolbar, holding the selected global tab's options and its own Reset button |
| Output format (*format*) | The aspect ratio of the canvas — the preview and every export — picked from the Format tab's thumbnails: Free, Twitter (1200:628, the default), Square 1:1, Portrait 4:5, Story 9:16, Landscape 16:9, Custom; not persisted, back to Twitter with the Global Resets and Clear all. One rule, `GridPreview.CanvasRatio` |
| Free format (*format libre*) | The output format taking, between 9:16 and 21:9, the ratio where the images lose the least — crops plus bands, weighted by the cells' area; texts and empty cells left out; held while a separator or a crop bar is dragged, or the wheel scales the crop's kept part |
| Custom format (*format personnalisé*) | The output format of the user's own, set in the **Width** and **Height** fields of the Format tab — changing one, or **Exact pixels**, picks it — or by dragging the canvas's edges and corners while that tab is selected, its green grips marking them, Ctrl keeping the center — its thumbnail labelled with its value, `3:2` or `1080×1350`: a ratio at the native size, or, with **Exact pixels**, the exact size of every export, 100 to 8192 px a side, reduced, ratio kept, only by a smaller PNG max size entry or Video size; its shape within 1:10 to 10:1; until first picked, the native size of the format in force, greyed; not persisted, back there with the Global Resets and Clear all |
| Format thumbnail (*miniature de format*) | One format in the Format tab's options: the current layout, its cells as resized, drawn schematically at the format's ratio, its name below; Free outlined dashed |
| Soundtrack (*bande son*) | The global effect mixing the sound of an audio or video file over the heard videos, at its own volume; it plays its **part** — **From** and **To** in the file, the whole file by default — on the grid's clock, **looped** or once, then silence; cut where the video ends, and its part gives a grid of stills its length |
| Fade (*fondu*) | The global effect bringing the grid's sound mix — the heard videos and the soundtrack — in from silence at the start of the video and out to silence at its end, over one duration (0.1 to 5 s, 1 s at first), on a Squared or Linear curve; halved on a video shorter than two fades; heard in the MP4 export and in the preview on every loop of the grid; off at start-up, disabled while nothing is heard |
| Borders (*bordures*) | The global effect drawing borders on the grid, off at start-up: the Corners style, or a line style drawn as an outer frame around the grid (on by default) and as inner lines filling a gap between the cells (off by default, the cells then touching), with the Twitter corners, every style at an opacity, in a color set from its options — hotpink by default, the last one chosen taken back at start-up |
| Corners style (*style Coins*) | The Borders' default style, the app's signature: an L-bracket over the images at each of the grid's four corners, each arm covering 10 % of its edge; no gap between the cells |
| Twitter corners (*coins Twitter*) | The Borders' option rounding the grid's four outer corners as Twitter / X rounds a posted image (3 % of the grid's longer side), the brackets and the outer frame following the curve; cut out in the preview only, the exports filling the rounded-off corners with the brackets or the frame, for Twitter to round; in the Twitter format only — disabled in the others, its setting kept; on by default, that default an app setting of the ⚙ menu |
| Gap (*espace*) | The space the Borders leave between the cells (every style but Corners, with its inner lines on), the cells shrinking for it; hit-testing still gives it to the cells beside it |
| Effects toolbar | The always-visible row of effect tabs, hanging below the options toolbar: the "Effects" label, the effect tabs, the Reset button at the far right |
| Effect tab (*onglet*) | One effect's tab in the effects toolbar, holding its activation checkbox; the selected tab is the one whose options show |
| Activation checkbox | The checkbox in an effect tab: checked while the effect is on for the selected cell |
| Eyedropper (*pipette*) | The first of a color option's two buttons — the Background's, the Borders', the Cutout's, a text layer's — the second, **Color…**, touching it, opening the standard color dialog, which the option follows live: clicked, it holds the grid and reads the preview's cells under the cursor, as the export draws them when it was clicked; the option follows the color live, a loupe shows the pixels and the hex value, a click takes it, Escape cancels. Its **sample** (1 to 5 px), the side of the square it averages, is chosen in the menu of its ▾, which arms it |
| Animations (*animations*) | The cell effect playing a motion in the cell on the grid's clock, its kind chosen in a Type drop-down — **Zoom** only for now: in to +20 % and back around the image's center, on a sine, one back-and-forth every 1 to 30 s (6 s by default). Its cycle is a playing loop: it counts in the video length and draws a progress line on a still. At time 0, its **starting state**, the one a PNG shows (`MotionEffect`, `ImageLook.Motion`) |
| Fit mode (*mode d'ajustement*) | The Zoom effect's **Contain** or **Fill** button pressed: the zoom computed from the cell and the image (`ImageLook.ZoomFit`, `ImageLook.ZoomIn`) — the whole image in the cell, or the cell covered — so it stays fitted when either changes shape; pressing it recenters the image, once; the wheel, the slider or unpressing it leave it for the free zoom it gave. An arriving image gets Contain's zoom as a free zoom, the mode unpressed |
| Angle (*angle*) | The Rotate effect's angle, −180° to +180°, set by its slider all the way round: the quarter turn last chosen — a thumbnail, Ctrl+R — plus the fine angle; a half turn reads +180° (`ImageLook.Angle`) |
| Fine angle | The part of the Rotate effect's angle beyond its quarter turn, within ±180° (`ImageLook.FineAngle`): the image turned that much around its cell's center after its quarter turn has been fitted, zoomed to keep covering its cell |
| Starting point | Where the Frames effect makes an animated image start playing — inside its trim, on any of its frames for a video or a GIF |
| Trim (*découpe*) | The Frames effect's **From** and **To** frames of a video or an animated GIF, both played: only the part between them plays — in the preview, the sound, the exports and the video length — looping on itself; the whole content by default. Set by a slider and, for a video, minutes : seconds : frame fields at the file's frame rate, for a GIF one frame-number field |
| Speed (*vitesse*) | The Frames effect's multiple of a content's own pace — a video's frame rate, a GIF's delays, a PDF's or a text's second a step — from ×0.25 to ×4 in steps of 0.25, ×1 by default: its loop divided by it, in the preview, the exports, the video length and the Cascade; a video's sound following it, its pitch with it; none while frozen |
| Frozen | An animated image the Frames effect holds on one frame, shown and exported as a still |
| Heard | A video whose sound is in the grid's mix: it has a sound track, plays (not frozen), and its Volume effect does not mute it |
| Video length (*durée de la vidéo*) | What the exported MP4 or GIF lasts: the **range**'s when one is set; else the longest playing loop — an Animations cycle counting as one — the shorter contents starting over until it ends; the soundtrack's part for a grid of stills with it on; none while the export is a PNG. One rule, `Animation.VideoLength`, read by the export, the preview's loop and the length readout |
| Range (*plage*) | The part of the grid's timeline the video plays and exports: a **start** and an **end**, set by the timeline's two handles or the transport's time fields, the end possibly past the grid's loop — the contents looping, a GIF repeated under a longer music; the preview loops on it, the Fade at its edges. **Keep range** checked, it is kept when the grid's loop changes; unchecked, it goes back to the whole loop. **Fit to soundtrack** sets it to one play of the soundtrack's part. Part of the undo history, reset by the Global Resets and Clear all |
| Length readout (*durée affichée*) | The `⏱ 12.5 s` label at the right end of the transport, always shown: the video length as the grid stands, `⏱ —` without one, the detail in its tooltip. A readout, not a control |
| Transport (*barre de lecture*) | The row under the preview playing the grid like a video player: the Play / Pause button (`Space`), the timeline, the video's time over the length readout, `3.2 s / ⏱ 10.0 s`, and under the timeline the range's start and end fields, **Keep range** and **Fit to soundtrack**; disabled without a video length and while exporting |
| Timeline (*ligne de temps*) | The transport's track, as long as the grid's loop — or the range's end plus one loop, when the range ends past it —, the range between two handles, dimmed outside, its played part filled up to a thumb at the grid time, a tick where each Cascade turn begins, a dashed one where the grid's loop ends; clicked, it moves the grid there within the range, dragged, it scrubs with scrub audio; a handle dragged moves the range's start or end, the end past the right edge lengthening it |
| Grid time (*temps de la grille*) | Where the grid stands in its loop, on the preview's one clock (`AnimationPlayer.GridTime`, `PlaybackClock`): the instant the exported video shows the same frame at; it stands still while paused, wraps at the end of the loop, and goes back to 0 when the grid starts over |
| Scrub audio (*extraits au glissement*) | The short snippets of the mix heard at the timeline's thumb while it is dragged, each ramped in and out |
| Grid background (*fond de grille*) | The global effect painting one fill — the shared fill — under the whole grid: the cells, the gap, the Twitter corners, a grid of layers only. Off at start-up; turned on, it turns off every cell's Background, turned off, it turns back on those it turned off; until first turned on, its color follows the first cell's automatic color; its **background image** drawn over the fill |
| Background image (*image de fond*) | The Grid background's image, video or GIF, over its fill and under the cells, across the whole grid: a floating cell as large as the grid, every cell effect applying to it; chosen by a drop onto the Grid background's options, Paste or Browse…; laid as **Fill**, **Contain**, **Stretch** or **Tile** — the tiles' size its zoom —, at an opacity and a zoom set in the options; arriving muted, with Fill; kept while the Grid background is off, removed by its Resets and Clear all; a grid holding it alone exported at its size |
| Background edit view (*vue d'édition du fond*) | The layer edit view, for the background image — opened by a double-click on its thumbnail or Edit: the image alone, at the grid's shape — its own, shown whole with a badge, while stretched or tiled —, every tool of a cell working on it; closed by Escape, Done or a double-click outside; preview only |
| Cascade (*cascade*) | The global effect playing the videos and animated GIFs one after the other, in cell order, instead of all at once, then starting over from the first: each plays its **turn**, standing still before it on its starting point and after it on the last frame it played, heard during its turn only; a **Pause** of 0 to 3 s after every turn; the other animated contents play alongside. Off at start-up; turned on or off, or its pause changed, it starts the grid over. One rule, `CascadeSchedule` |
| Turn (*tour*) | What a content plays in the Cascade: one whole loop of its played part at its speed, from its starting point round to it again, beginning when the previous turn and its pause end |
| Layers (*calques*) | The global effect laying **layers** over the whole grid, the last one on top: off at start-up, with none; they take the mouse only while its tab is selected, Ctrl+V and a drop on the grid filling the cells as ever; files dropped onto its tab become layers; an empty grid holds them too, its canvas transparent around them; reset — every layer removed — by its Resets and Clear all. One rule, `GridLayers` |
| Layer (*calque*) | An image, a GIF, a video or a text laid over the grid, free of the cells: a **floating cell** — its content, the cell effects of its look, its box — placed in fractions of the grid, its box taking the shape of its content as shown; selected first, then dragged, resized by the wheel, turned by its round handle — the cursor a rotation arrow over it — or Shift + wheel, raised or lowered, duplicated — the copy just above it, a little off, or left behind by a Ctrl + drag —, hidden from the layer list; its Background off by default. While one is selected, the effect tabs act on it |
| Layer guide (*guide de placement*) | A dashed fluorescent green line across the whole grid while a layer is dragged: the grid's edges and center, every cell's, and the other layers' — fainter, the layer it comes from outlined dashed — the dragged layer's edges or center coming near it or resting on it, the upright rectangle around a turned layer counting; the layer snaps onto it within 6 px, Shift ignoring them all; one line per position, however many layers share it; preview only |
| Layer list (*liste des calques*) | The left of the Layers options, as Paint.NET's: one row per layer, the top one first — its thumbnail, its name, its visibility checkbox —, its selection the selected layer's, and icon buttons below: Add image…, Paste, Add text, Copy, Duplicate, Edit, Delete, To back, Lower, Raise, To front; a right click on a row opens the layer's menu |
| Layer edit view (*vue d'édition du calque*) | What the preview shows while a layer is edited — opened by a double-click on it or Edit, closed by Escape, Done or a double-click outside: the layer alone, upright, as one large cell over the whole working surface, every tool of a cell working on it as usual; the grid given back as it was, the layer keeping its new effects; preview only |
| Text layer (*calque texte*) | A layer drawing a text — several lines, its emojis in their own colors, flags included — in a font, bold, italic or in capitals or not, its lines aligned left, centered, right or justified, its size automatic or a **box** its lines wrap in — its width dragged by its side handles, half the grid at first —, a color, with an outline, a shadow and a background box; typed in the Layers options; its size a line's height. Drawn by Direct2D, which GDI+ cannot replace for the emojis |
| Text style (*style de texte*) | A text layer's look under a name — font, emoji font, bold, italic, ALL CAPS, alignment, color, outline, shadow, box; never its text, size, size mode or place: the built-in ones (Default, Meme, Subtitle, Label, Neon, Subtle) and the user's, saved from the selected text by **Save style…** and kept in `text-styles.json` next to the exe; applied from the strip above the text's settings, the last one applied given to a new text |
| Flag font (*police des drapeaux*) | Twemoji Mozilla cut down to its flags, embedded in the app: what draws the flags of the texts — a text layer's and a text cell's — in color, 🇪🇺 or 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿, which Windows' emoji font draws as letters; whatever the text's font |
| Emoji font (*police d'emojis*) | The font a text layer's emojis are drawn in, picked in the **Emoji** drop-down beside its font, the letters staying in that font: Segoe UI Emoji, Windows' own, by default; an emoji font installed — a color font holding 😀 —, or Twemoji, Noto Color Emoji, OpenMoji and Blobmoji, installed and updated from the Component updates window into `emoji-fonts\` next to the exe, never installed into Windows; its flags drawn too, but the four the flag font joins; part of a text style |
| Text shadow (*ombre du texte*) | A text layer's soft shadow under its letters and their outline: a **Halo** all around them, or a **Drop** down to the right; its size a share of a line |
| Start over (*repartir de zéro*) | Every animated image playing again from its starting point at one instant, the soundtrack from its beginning: what an image arriving in a cell, or one removed, does to the grid |
| Undo history (*historique d'annulation*) | The last 50 **steps** — fewer when the images they park, gone from the grid, weigh more than 512 MB together, the oldest steps dropped first — walked back with Ctrl+Z and forward again with Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z; a new change drops what could be redone; empty at each launch, not persisted. One rule, `GridHistory` (see RULES.md) |
| Step (*étape*) | One entry of the undo history: the whole composed state — the cells' images and looks, the layout and its separators, the format, the global effects, the decisions on the people — as it stood once it settled for ~300 ms with no mouse button held; a gesture or a wheel burst is one step. The selection, the tabs and the app settings are not part of it |
| Options toolbar | The always-visible row above the effects toolbar, holding the selected tab's options and the effect's own Reset button |
| Feature line (*ligne de fonctionnalité*) | One feature of a tab's options on a line of its own — its checkbox, or its caption, then its controls, each slider with its name and value above it — the lines running top-down in the options toolbar, a new column to the right when its height is full, scrolled sideways when wider than the window; the Background, the Frames and the Layers so far |
| Option thumbnail (*miniature d'option*) | A choice among values in a cell effect's options — a Background fill, a Crop ratio, Contain or Fill, a quarter turn, a mirror, a Blur kind — drawn in the Background's format: a pictogram in a 56 × 40 box, the cell grey and the image white, its name below; pressed ones highlighted. An action among them (Crop's 100 %) is set apart by a line. One control, `ThumbnailStrip` |
| Helper indicator (*indicateur d'aide*) | A measure or geometry aid drawn over a cell in the preview only — guides, handles, value readouts such as the zoom percentage; always fluorescent green (see RULES.md) |
| Icon (*icône*) | The picture of a control that does something — a menu entry (never a caption nor a submenu's values), a tab, a button, a drop-down's entry: an emoji in full color wherever one says the thing, the app's drawing otherwise; one per action, wherever it shows; a checked menu toggle shows it framed instead of a ✓; an emoji once drawn kept in `icon-cache\` next to the exe, so a later start reads it, a first one showing it an instant after the window. Formerly the *menu icon* (see RULES.md) |
| Position readout (*indicateur de position*) | The helper indicator showing how far an image is from its cell's center, in the pixels of the export: an X cross on the image's center, a faint dashed line to it from the cell's center, and `x -35px` over `y +12px` in the cell's top-right corner, below the zoom badge when it shows, x to the right, y downward; the cell's ✥ handle hidden meanwhile; shown whenever that offset changes with the image staying in its cell — a move, a wheel zoom, a turn, a crop, a separator, the format, an undo — held 1 s, or while the gesture lasts, then faded; preview only |
| Progress line (*ligne de progression*) | The helper indicator along the bottom edge of every playing cell — a still moved by its Animations effect playing the motion's cycle: a fluorescent green line growing from the left edge as the content's loop plays, continuous, starting over at each loop; none on a frozen content, nor under the selected cell's blur or crop bars; preview only |
| File explorer (*explorateur de fichiers*) | The collapsible panel at the right of the preview, as wide as its splitter was dragged: a search box over the base folder's index — `*` alone listing every file, in the **sort order** — the favorites while the box is empty, shown as tiles filling their rows, a few pages loaded at a time — as many per row as fit at the tile size; its tiles are dragged into the cells like files from the Explorer; its **search criteria** choose where the words are looked for. Five views, picked in its **views drop-down** — each entry after its icon — or cycled with Ctrl+F pressed again in the search box: the **folder view**, the **People view**, the **search view** and the two **GIPHY views**; every start opens on the search view's favorites |
| Search view (*vue recherche*) | The file explorer's view over the whole index: the favorites while the search box is empty, every file for `*`, else the matches |
| Search criteria (*critères de recherche*) | The file explorer's **🔠** and **💡** toggle buttons, between the views drop-down and the search box: where the typed words are looked for — the file's name and subfolders, the text inside the file, or both, both at start-up; at least one stays on; with 💡 alone, no folder is found; not remembered |
| Folder view (*vue dossiers*) | The file explorer's view browsing the base folder folder by folder: the **open folder**'s subfolders A→Z, then its files in the **sort order**, as the disk holds them; a search there looks only below the open folder, the matching folders first; its open folder remembered between sessions |
| Open folder (*dossier ouvert*) | The folder the folder view shows, relative to the base folder — the base folder itself at first, and again when the search box's key cycles back to the folder view; one gone from the disk gives way to its nearest parent still there |
| Folder tile (*tuile de dossier*) | A folder in the folder view's grid: Windows' thumbnail of the folder, or a drawn folder, filling the tile over the panel's background, its name in bold on the folder's front flap over a soft shadow — the leading words it shares with a sibling folder drawn small above the rest, `Outils RS ›` over `Alpha` — the number of files below it as the index counts them under the tile; double-clicked, it opens; no heart, no drag |
| List section (*section*) | A run of the file explorer's tiles under a titled line with its count — `Outils RS (25)`: in the folder view, one per group of folders sharing their first words, then **Other folders**, one per **series**, **Named files**, one per **name pattern** and one per source found by itself, while 🗂️ **Folder groups** is on; in a search, **Path** then **Content**, while 🗃️ **Search groups** is on. Both buttons on by default, remembered between sessions |
| Name pattern (*motif de nom*) | A kind of file name a machine made up — Camera, Screenshots, Messaging, AI, a known source (ssstwitter.com, FaceApp…), Generic names, Identifiers, Short codes, Date stamps, Timestamps, Numbers — a list section of the folder view of its own, after the named files; a source found by itself — a word shared by 3 files or more, then a date, a number or a code — gets one too, last. Each turned off in the ⚙ menu's **File explorer groups**, its files going to the next pattern they fit, else among the named files |
| Series (*série*) | 3 named files or more of a folder sharing their first words, ignoring case and accents — `marlène schiappa …`, `homme soja …` — a list section of their own before the named files, titled by the words they share; turned off by the 📚 **Series** button or **Series** in the ⚙ menu's **File explorer groups** |
| Breadcrumb (*fil d'Ariane*) | The folder view's caption line: the ↑ button, the base folder's name and every folder down to the open one, each opening when clicked, then what the list holds; in the People view, `People` then the group open |
| People view (*vue Personnes*) | The file explorer's view of the faces found in the base folder's images: the **face groups** — named A→Z, then the larger first — a *Hidden* tile for the hidden ones, a *Low quality* tile for the **low-quality faces**, then the **faces alone**; a search filters the groups by name. Fed by the **face pass**, while the **face analysis** — off by default, an app setting of the ⚙ menu — is on |
| Face group (*groupe de visages*) | A person in the People view: faces found close, or that the user put together — its best face over two stacked cards, its number of files, its name in bold or *Unnamed*; opened, its **sub-groups** — the groups merged into it, *Un-merge* bringing one back — then every file of it and of them, under its name as a title, clicked to rename it. Reached from a photo too: the **People** submenu of a file's tile lists the groups of its faces — a face alone made a person as it opens. Renamed, hidden, merged by dragging it onto another (after a confirmation); a face dragged onto it joins it, *Not <name>* on a file takes its face out for good. The decisions kept in `people.json` |
| Face alone (*visage seul*) | A clear face close to no other: listed after the groups as a file tile showing the face cut out of its file; dragged onto a group, it joins it |
| Low-quality face (*visage de faible qualité*) | A poor face — small, unsure or turned — close to no other: set apart in the People view's *Low quality* tile, opened like the *Hidden* one, rather than listed with the faces alone |
| Face quality (*qualité d'un visage*) | How good a picture of its person a face makes: its detection score, its size in pixels and how frontal it is, together. A poorer face weighs less in a group and must be closer to join one |
| Face grouping (*regroupement des visages*) | How alike faces must be to be one person: an app setting of the ⚙ menu — Very loose, Loose, **Normal** (the default), Strict, Very strict; stricter mixes fewer people, looser splits fewer. Changing it groups the faces again at once, the user's decisions kept |
| Face pass (*passe visages*) | The reading of the faces in every image and PDF page of the index, right after the Windows OCR pass, with its own pink bar: YuNet finds them, SFace gives each an embedding; cached in `index\files.faces` |
| Face engine (*moteur de visages*) | YuNet and SFace, two OpenCV Zoo models: never shipped, installed from the Component updates window into `face-engine\` next to the exe, running on the OCR engine's ONNX Runtime |
| Visual analysis (*analyse visuelle*) | What the base folder's images show, looked at on this computer — their **tags**, the **similarity tier** of the searches under 💡, **Find similar images**: an app setting of the ⚙ menu, off by default, fed by the **Vision pass**; *Forget visual analysis…* deletes what it found, the captions included |
| Vision pass (*passe vision*) | The reading of every image and PDF page of the index by the **vision engine**, right after the face pass, with its own teal bar: an embedding per image, cached in `index\files.vision`, and its tags |
| Vision engine (*moteur de vision*) | SigLIP 2 base — a vision model and a text model sharing one space, and its tokenizer: never shipped, installed from the Component updates window into `vision-engine\` next to the exe, running on the OCR engine's ONNX Runtime; it also embeds the queries |
| Tag (*étiquette*) | A concept of the bilingual vocabulary close to what an image shows — `chien dog`, `plage beach` — its French and English words written into the image's Tags slot; the vocabulary shipped, plus the user's own in `vision-tags.txt` next to the exe; found under 💡 after every word read, a whole word before a word found inside another |
| Caption (*description*) | A sentence in English saying what an image shows — *A soccer player kicking a ball on a field* — written by the **Caption pass**, found under 💡 like the tags and shown in the Details view |
| Caption pass (*passe descriptions*) | The last indexing pass, with its own gold bar, while **Captions** — an app setting of the ⚙ menu, off by default — is on: Florence-2 base, the **caption engine**, installed from the Component updates window into `vision-engine\`, describes every image and PDF page in a few seconds each |
| Similarity tier (*palier de similarité*) | The last results of a search under 💡 while the visual analysis is on: the images that stand out for the query, whatever its language — at most 100, often none — the farthest first whatever the sort order, those found already left out, each with its **closeness** |
| Closeness (*proximité*) | How clearly an image found by its similarity stands out from the others, 0 to 100 % — the same scale for Find similar images and the similarity tier: a label at the tile's bottom left, before the size in the Details view, a line of the tooltip |
| Similar images setting (*réglage Similar images*) | How clearly an image must stand out to be listed by Find similar images and the similarity tier: an app setting of the ⚙ menu — Very loose, Loose, **Normal** (the default), Strict, Very strict; stricter shows fewer, closer images |
| Tile tooltip (*infobulle de tuile*) | What shows over a file's tile in the file explorer: its path — after a preset's description — then what the index holds of it, each after its icon: 💡 its caption, 🏷 its tags, 📝 the beginning of the text read, 👨‍🦰 the people its faces belong to |
| Similar images (*images similaires*) | The images of the index that stand out for a given one — itself left out, at most 100, `nothing close enough` when none does — listed in the search view with their closeness — `Similar to <name>`, its × giving the search back: **Find similar images** in a tile's or a cell's context menu, or an image dropped onto the search box |
| Tile (*tuile*) | One file in the file explorer's grid: its thumbnail enlarged or reduced to fill a 4:3 box — the row's width shared by the row's tiles — over white and grey squares showing its transparency, the heart in a medallion at its corner, its name below |
| Tile size (*taille des tuiles*) | The nominal width of a tile in the file explorer, 100 to 1 000 px, set by the slider at the bottom of the panel or Ctrl + wheel over the tiles: the width at which one more tile fits on a row, the tiles stretched to fill it; remembered between sessions |
| Sort order (*ordre de tri*) | How the file explorer orders its files, picked in the drop-down at the end of the tile size row: Date ↓ (the most recently created first, the default), Name A→Z, Size ↑ (the smallest first, an unknown size last). It orders `*` and a browsed folder alone; a search keeps its **relevance tiers** — the words in the file name (more first), the subfolders only, the content — each ranking the words found whole, then at a word's start, before the rest, and orders each of these. The favorites and the folders keep their own order; remembered between sessions |
| Details view (*vue Détails*) | The file explorer's display picked by the 📃 button after the sort drop-down, the 🔳 one giving back the tiles (**Thumbnails**, the default): one row per file — its thumbnail, a quarter of the tile size, its name, its size (`412 KB` in English, `412 Ko` in French, base 1024), and below for a file found by its content the words around the one found, as many as the row holds, the search's words highlighted in yellow; a folder's row, its name and file count over a strip of thumbnails of the files directly in it, in the sort order, a picture only. Every list follows it; remembered between sessions |
| Load (*chargement*) | The tiles a file explorer list adds at a time: **pages per load** (1 to 10, 2 by default, an app setting of the ⚙ menu) times a **page** — what the list shows at once — its last slot the **Loading…** tile while more remain, the next load coming when that tile is scrolled into view |
| Base folder (*dossier de base*) | The folder the file explorer indexes, with its subfolders; an app setting of the ⚙ menu |
| Secondary source (*source secondaire*) | Another folder — another drive, a NAS — shown in a color of its own (blue, violet, teal, green, plum: its tile, its name in the breadcrumb, the status line while it is open) at the top of the base folder in the folder view, in a *Secondary sources* section, with an index of its own in `index\secondary-sources\`: its list of files read when it is opened, its files searched only while it is open, its content analysed by 🔄 inside it — leaving it pauses the analysis —; its faces in the People view, its images among the similar images once analysed. Added and removed in the ⚙ menu's **Secondary sources…** window, never overlapping the base folder nor another source; a removed one's index deleted |
| Index (*index*) | The list of every file under the base folder with its creation date and size, cached in `index\files.index` next to the exe, loaded at start-up and rescanned in the background or with 🔄; what the search reads, never the disk — the files' content texts, read by the OCR passes, cached next to it in `index\files.content` |
| OCR pass (*passe OCR*) | One reading of the images and PDF pages' text, run over every such file in turn after indexing: **Windows OCR**, **RapidOCR**, then Windows OCR on the **Inverted** colours and on the **Ring** unrolled — the face, vision and caption passes taking their turns among them; each adds its words, each has its progress bar. Its words are **sure** or **weak** — the Ring's, and RapidOCR's read with a low confidence — the weak ones ranking a file found by them alone last |
| OCR engine (*moteur OCR*) | RapidOCR, the second pass's engine: never shipped, installed from the Component updates window — its models and its native libraries — into `ocr-engine\` next to the exe, the window opened on it when the content search is turned on without it |
| Spaces-ignored match (*correspondance sans espaces*) | A query word of 3 characters or more found in an OCR text once its spaces are removed — letters the OCR read apart, `S U P P O R T` found by *support*; ranked after an ordinary match |
| Favorite (*favori*) | A file hearted in the file explorer, or dropped onto it — from the Explorer, or a cell released there by its ✥ handle — with effects, by its dialog's **File only**; kept in `favorites.json` next to the exe with the effect presets, shown — all of them, the newest first — while the search box is empty |
| Pasted favorite (*favori collé*) | A cell with no file — a pasted image, a pasted or dropped text — made a favorite: saved first into `favorites-from-pasted\` next to the exe (the image as a PNG, the text as it came), the cell taking that file as its own; un-hearted, its file goes to the Recycle Bin once no effect preset holds it |
| Effect preset (*preset d'effets*) | A favorite of a new kind: a file, the effects turned on in its cell — the Background turned off too, the Zoom once changed since arrival — and a free-text **description**; saved by **⭐ Preset…** in the effects toolbar or a cell with effects released over the file explorer by its ✥ handle; several per file, each its own tile with a ★ FX badge, the description's first line under the file's name; dragged or double-clicked into a cell, the file arrives with those effects; found by a search (🔠) by its file's name or its description; kept in `favorites.json`. One rule, `LookPreset` |
| Second title (*titre secondaire*) | The text given with `--title` on the command line, shown after the app's name in the window title bar — `Image Grid Fusion — Undo / redo` — and on a second line of the tray icon's tooltip, to tell instances running side by side apart; Claude Code passes its session's name, status marker removed. Process-only, never remembered |
| Component (*composant*) | A file or a tool the app downloads rather than ships, from one upstream — a Hugging Face repository, a GitHub release, a NuGet package, a winget package: an engine's models, a runtime they run on, yt-dlp or a companion, an emoji font. Every one is installed and updated from the **Component updates window** |
| Component updates window (*fenêtre Component updates*) | The window listing every component, grouped by kind — Engines, Runtimes, Web tools, Emoji fonts — each with its name a link to its upstream's page, what it is for in its ℹ️ tooltip, its version installed, its size on the disk and when it was installed, its **pinned** and **forced** versions, its latest version — checked when the window opens and on Check now, a spinner meanwhile — and its date; **Install**, **Update**, ▾ *Back to the forced / pinned version* or *Uninstall…* on its row, its 📂 opening Explorer on its files, several at once, each with its progress bar, or **Install all**, **Update all** and **Uninstall all**; the total on the disk under the list. Opened from the ⚙ menu's **Component updates…**, and by a feature lacking a component, its rows highlighted and a balloon saying why. Closed while one installs, it asks first; a file in use is replaced at the next start, a restart offered |
| Pinned version (*version épinglée*) | The version of a component the app's code pins, its files' URLs and SHA-256 with it: what a first install takes and *Back to the pinned version* gives back; copied into `components.json` at every start, the code always winning |
| Forced version (*version forcée*) | A version of a component written by hand into `components.json` — its files' URLs and SHA-256, or a winget tool's version —: a safe fallback without rebuilding the app, given back by *Back to the forced version*; never written by the app |
| Components manifest (*manifeste des composants*) | `components.json` next to the exe: per component its pinned and forced versions, the version installed with its files' hashes, the one waiting for the next start, when it was installed and last updated; recreated when missing |
| Shortcuts window (*fenêtre des raccourcis*) | The window listing every key and mouse gesture of the app and what it does, by where it works — read from `SHORTCUTS.md`, embedded in the exe, so the app and GitHub show the same list; opened by **Keyboard shortcuts…** in the ⚙ menu or its key ([SHORTCUTS.md](SHORTCUTS.md#anywhere)), modeless, beside the main window |
| Last video (*dernière vidéo*) | The MP4 or GIF the last animated Copy generated in the session, its file kept in the temp folder: put back on the clipboard by **Copy last MP4 / GIF**, saved elsewhere by **Save last…**, whatever the grid has become; replaced by the next animated Copy, forgotten at exit. Its tooltips end with the memory peak its export reached. App state, not an effect |
| UI language (*langue de l'interface*) | The language of the app's texts — **English**, the default, or **French** — picked in the ⚙ menu's **Language / Langue**, each named in its own language; applied when the app starts again, a restart offered; the numbers shown follow it (`12.5 s` / `12,5 s`, `412 KB` / `412 Ko`) and the shortcuts window shows the list in it. An app setting; one rule, `Resources/Strings.resx` (see RULES.md § UI Language) |
| Video size (*taille des vidéos*) | The longer side an MP4 or GIF export's canvas is clamped to — 1920 px (Full HD) by default, 2560 or 4096 — an app setting of the ⚙ menu, so a video's frames stay light in memory and quick to encode; the PNG goes up to 8192 (see RULES.md § Export Memory) |
| Native size (*taille native*) | The size of the PNG Copy and Save give: the smallest canvas where no image is downscaled as drawn in its cell — its zoom, its fine angle, the Borders' gap — its longer side within [1200, 8192]; a PDF page, a thumbnail or a text never widening it; the Custom format's exact pixels replace it. Shown in their tooltip |
| PNG max size (*taille max du PNG*) | The entries of the Copy and Save ▾ menus — 720p, 1080p, 2K, 4K, 8K — copying or saving at once a PNG whose longer side is at most 1280, 1920, 2560, 3840 or 7680 px, never enlarged past the native size; each shows the size it gives |
| URL zone (*zone URL*) | The file explorer's last row: a URL field and its **Load** button, a preview at the left showing a loader then the media's thumbnail, a caption saying where the load stands; the media behind the URL — an X post's first photo or video, a direct link's image or video, another site's through yt-dlp — downloaded into the temp folder, then dragged into a cell like a tile. Not remembered, out of the undo history |
| Resolver (*résolveur*) | One way of finding the media behind a URL, tried in order by `UrlResolver`: the X / Twitter post, the direct link to a media, then yt-dlp — optional, installed with winget into `yt-dlp\` next to the exe on the user's **Yes**, with its **companions**, Deno and ffmpeg, in `deno\` and `ffmpeg\`, for the largest formats up to the Video size |
| Online library (*bibliothèque en ligne*) | A web service of media searched from the file explorer, in a view of its own — GIPHY the first — its results downloaded as files before they reach the grid. Asked for first as *external sources*: *source* is a file's origin read from its name (see Name pattern) |
| GIPHY view (*vue GIPHY*) | One of the file explorer's two views of GIPHY, the first online library — **GIPHY GIFs** and **GIPHY Stickers**, each shown or hidden from the ⚙ menu's **GIPHY** submenu: searched on Enter with the user's own API key — that submenu's **GIPHY API key…** — Enter on an empty box or its **Trending** button giving the trending, nothing asked of GIPHY before: entered, a view shows its last list of the session, else invites to search; 50 results a call — a free key allowing 100 calls an hour, the app counting its own and its breadcrumb showing them — their thumbnails playing; a click downloads a result, then it is dragged like a file — a GIF as its MP4, a sticker as a GIF — a double-click adds it; hearted, its file kept in `giphy-favorites\` next to the exe; **Powered by GIPHY** at the end of its breadcrumb; skipped by Ctrl+F while no key is set |
| Content rating (*classification*) | The most a GIPHY search may show — G, PG, PG-13, or R, the default, with no filter — an app setting of the ⚙ menu |
| Cell copy (*copie de cellule*) | One cell's image, or one layer, copied alone — `Ctrl+C` with it selected, **Copy cell** in a cell's menu, **Copy** in the layer list: rendered with every effect of its cell but the Background, at its native size, cut to the image as the cell shows it, transparent around it — a turned layer turned, a playing content's frame shown —, a PNG on the clipboard to paste elsewhere or into another cell as a plain image. `Ctrl+Shift+C` copies the grid. One rule, `CellCopy` |
| Previous copy (*copie précédente*) | The last content a Copy put on the clipboard — PNG, MP4, GIF or JPEG for sharing — kept as a file in `previous\` next to the exe, the one before deleted; named after the cells' files joined by ` + `, `fusion-…` when none has one. A copy of the clipboard's file, not that file itself |
