# Layers

> Working document — images and texts laid over the whole grid as free **layers**, each one moved,
> resized, rotated and stacked, an emoji being a mere character of a text, drawn in color.
> This file is the source of truth for the planned work until implemented,
> then the log of every adjustment made to it afterwards.

---

## Overview

A new **global effect**, **Layers**, lays **layers** over the whole grid: an **image layer** (a
picture pasted or dropped) or a **text layer** (any text, its emojis drawn in full color). Each
layer is free on the grid — it may straddle several cells — and is moved, resized, rotated, and
raised or lowered among the others. **Every cell effect applies to a layer** (Q&A #12): a layer is a
**floating cell** — its content and its `ImageLook` — and the effects toolbar acts on the selected
layer as it does on the selected cell.

It supersedes `workfiles/20260927-emoji-stickers.md` (cancelled): an emoji is no longer a feature of
its own but a character of a text layer.

**Nothing changes for the cells** unless the Layers tab is selected (Q&A #5): the layers take the
mouse, the keyboard, a paste or a drop **only while that tab is selected** in the Global toolbar.
Otherwise they are drawn, and the cells behave exactly as today.

| Undo history | `UI/GridHistory.cs` — `GlobalState` gets `GridLayers Layers, bool LayersOn`, `Describe` its label; `MainForm.CaptureState` / `RestoreState` |

## Nature — a Global Effect

The layers belong to the **grid**, not to a cell + image pair (RULES.md § Global Effects).

| Event | Layers |
|---|---|
| An image is replaced, deleted, swapped; a cell *Reset* button | Untouched — they stay where they are on the grid |
| The layout changes, a separator is dragged, the format changes | Kept, at the same place in fractions of the grid |
| *Clear all*, the Layers options' own *Reset*, the Global tabs' *Reset* | Reset — no layer left (the initial state) |
| Its activation checkbox unchecked | Hidden from the preview and the exports, **kept**, the selected layer deselected; checked again, they come back as they were |
| Export running | The Global toolbar is locked; no layer can be selected nor edited |
| Undo / redo | Every change of the layers is a step; a drag or a wheel burst is one step; the selected layer is out of the history |

- **Unavailable while the grid holds no image** — nothing to lay them over: its checkbox and options
  disabled, the checkbox's tooltip saying why (Iteration 6).
- **Off at start-up**, with no layer. Its tab holds an activation checkbox; its options show whenever
  the tab is selected; **adding a layer activates it** (acting on any option).
- **Not persisted.**
- Geometry in **fractions of the grid**: centre (x, y) in fractions of the grid's width and height,
  size in fractions of the grid's **shorter side** (the layer's longer side), angle in degrees.

## Layer Mode — the Layers Tab Selected

Agreed (Q&A #5): the layers act **only while the Layers tab is selected** in the Global toolbar, so
the layer mode never interferes with the cells.

| While the Layers tab is… | Mouse over a layer | Mouse elsewhere | Ctrl+V, a drop on the grid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selected (and the effect on, or turned on by an addition) | The layer's gestures | The cells' gestures, as today | The cells, as today (Iterations 8, 9) |
| Not selected | Ignored — a click reaches the cell under it | The cells, as today | The cells, as today |

- **Ctrl+V and a drop on the grid never make a layer** (Q&A #24, Iteration 8): layers are added from the
  Layers options — or by a drop **onto the Layers tab or its options** (§ Image Layer).

- Selecting another Global tab, or turning the effect off, **deselects** the layer.
- **Exclusive selections** (carried over, emoji stickers' Q&A #11): selecting a layer clears the
  cell selection; selecting a cell clears the layer selection.

### Gestures

Carried over from the emoji stickers (their Q&A #3, #8, #9): a layer is **selected first**.

| Gesture | On | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Click, or a drag started on it | An **unselected** layer | Selects it only — it does not move |
| Drag | The **selected** layer | Moves it |
| Wheel, pointer over it | The selected layer | Resizes it, **5 % a notch** like the resizable zones (proposed), between 3 % and 100 % of the grid's shorter side |
| **Shift** + wheel, pointer over it | The selected layer | Rotates it, 5° a notch, free angle (Q&A #10) — Ctrl and Alt keep their meanings for the wheel |
| Delete | — | Removes the selected layer |
| Escape, click outside every layer | — | Deselects the layer |
| Drag the **rotation handle** | The selected layer | Turns it around its center, following the pointer; **Shift** snaps to every 15° (Q&A #25) |
| Double-click | A layer | Opens its **edit view** (§ Edit View) |

- The wheel acts on the selected layer only while the pointer is over it; elsewhere it zooms the
  cell under the pointer, as today.
- Hit-testing uses the layer's **rotated rectangle**; the layer on top wins where several overlap.
- A moved layer keeps its **centre inside the grid**; the part outside is clipped at the grid edge.

### Stacking Order

Agreed (Q&A #5): the selected layer is **raised or lowered** among the others.

- **Selecting a layer no longer brings it to the front** — that would undo the order set by hand
  (revises the emoji stickers' Q&A #12).
- A new layer is added **on top**.
- The Layers options hold **Raise**, **Lower** (one place up or down), **To front** and **To back**
  (Q&A #11), disabled where they would not move the selected layer.

- The stacking is done **from the layer list** (§ Layer List): its icon buttons **To back**, **Lower**,
  **Raise**, **To front** — no text, their names in tooltips — replace the text buttons (Iteration 7).

### Selection Indicator

- The selected layer is outlined by its rotated rectangle in the **helper colour**
  (`HelperColor` / `HelperHalo`), drawn in `GridPreview.OnPaint` only — never in the exports.
- Its **rotation handle**: a round knob above the middle of its top edge, joined to it by a short
  line, in the helper colour, white while hovered or dragged (Q&A #25, Iteration 7). It turns with the
  layer, and is grabbed on its own area before the layer's.

### Layer List

At the left of the Layers options, as Paint.NET's layers panel (Iteration 7, Q&A #25):

- One row per layer, **the top layer first**: a thumbnail of its content over the grey and white squares,
  its name — the file's name, the text's first line, *Pasted image* — and a **visibility checkbox**.
- Its selection **is** the selected layer, both ways: clicking a row selects that layer on the grid (the
  cell deselected); selecting a layer on the grid selects its row.
- Under it, a row of **icon buttons without text**, their names in tooltips: *Add image…*, *Paste*,
  *Add text*, *Edit* (§ Edit View), *Delete*, *To back*, *Lower*, *Raise*, *To front* — each disabled
  where it would do nothing.
- A row unchecked **hides its layer** in the preview and every export, kept with its settings: it is
  neither drawn, played, heard nor counted in the video length, and takes no click on the grid. The
  Layers tab's checkbox still hides or shows them all at once. A layer's visibility is part of the undo
  history.
- The **global options toolbar grows taller** to show about four rows (Q&A #25) — one height for every
  Global tab, as RULES.md § Global Toolbar asks; the text's settings stay beside the list.
- Files **dropped onto the Layers tab or its options** — from Windows' Explorer or the app's file
  explorer — are laid over the grid as layers, like *Add image…*; dropped elsewhere on the Global
  toolbars, a file still becomes the soundtrack.

## Layer Kinds

### Image Layer

- **Whatever a cell accepts** (Q&A #7): a still picture, an animated GIF, a video — its content kept
  as a `SourceImage` (`ImageLoader.FromImage` for a pasted bitmap, the file loader for a file),
  never put in a cell.
- **Added from the Layers options** (Q&A #6, revised by Iterations 8 and 9):
  - the **Add image…** (a file picker) and **Paste** icon buttons of the layer list — a pasted text
    makes a **text layer**;
  - a **drop onto the Layers tab or its options** (a file from Windows' Explorer, an explorer tile);
  - **Ctrl+V and a drop on the grid fill the cells as ever**, whatever tab is selected.
  - The ✥ handle's swap is untouched: it moves cells, never makes a layer.
- Its **box follows the content as shown** (Q&A #16): the proportions of `ImageLook.Shown` —
  cropped, then turned — so a crop or a quarter turn reshapes it; its size is its longer side. No
  band shows inside a layer.
- **An animated layer plays on the grid's clock** (RULES.md § Transport): from its starting point
  at the clock's origin, looping on its played part — its **Frames** effect's trim, starting point
  and freeze, like a cell's (§ Cell Effects on a Layer). Its loop
  **counts in the video length** (`Animation.VideoLength` / `Animation.LoopOf` take the layers'
  loops); the preview (`AnimationPlayer`) and the export's frames (`GridExport`) both read it there.
- **The Cascade** leaves the layers out: they play alongside, like the contents taking no turn
  (proposed).
- **Its sound**: a video layer's sound joins the grid's mix (preview `PreviewSound`, export
  `MixedSound`), under the Fade like the rest, set by its **Volume** effect (Q&A #12).
- An image layer leaving the grid (deleted, reset, undone) goes through `GridPreview.ReleaseImage`
  like a cell's, so the undo history keeps it alive and counts it in its parked bytes.

### Text Layer

- Any text, several lines allowed; its emojis drawn in **full color**, nothing emoji-specific in the
  app (the user's 2026-10-10 request).
- **Typed in the options** (Q&A #9): an **Add text** button creates a text layer — on top, at the
  grid's centre — selected, its **multi-line field** in the Layers options focused; the field edits
  the selected text layer, live; Win+. opens the Windows emoji panel. An empty text is drawn as
  nothing and stays selectable by its frame while selected.
- **Its settings** (Q&A #8), shown in the options for the selected text layer, disabled for an image
  layer or none:

  | Setting | Default (proposed) |
  |---|---|
  | **Font** (installed families) and **Bold** | Segoe UI, bold |
  | **Color** — the color split button, eyedropper and Color… (RULES.md § Color Options); the emojis keep their own colors | White |
  | **Outline** — on / off, its color (split button) and thickness | On, black, 8 % of the text height |
  | **Background** — on / off, its color (split button) and opacity, a box behind the text with a margin | Off, black, 50 % |
- Its size is its **text height** (a line's), in fractions of the grid's shorter side — 12 % for a
  new text, 40 % of its longer side for a new image —; the wheel scales it like any layer.

## Edit View

The crop could not be set on a layer (Iteration 7). A layer's effects get an **edit view** where every
tool of a cell works as usual (Q&A #25):

- **Opened on demand**: a double-click on a layer, or the list's *Edit* icon, while the Layers tab is
  selected. **Closed** by Escape, the *Done* icon that replaces *Edit*, or a double-click outside the
  layer; selecting another Global tab or the effect turned off close it too.
- Meanwhile the preview shows **the layer alone, as one large cell over the whole working surface**:
  the canvas holds a single cell shaped like the layer's box, fitted as large as the canvas allows,
  over the grey and white squares — the grid is not drawn. The layer is **upright**: its angle is set
  aside, as Q&A #14 wanted.
- In it, the layer **is** that cell, selected: the effects toolbar acts on it, and every gesture of a
  cell works — the blur's bars, the crop's edit view and its bars, the wheel's zoom, the drag moving
  the content within the box, the guides. What it changes is the layer's look, kept when the view
  closes; the layer's place and angle are left untouched.
- The view is **preview only**; an export started meanwhile draws the grid with its layers. Every
  change is part of the undo history as on a cell.

## Cell Effects on a Layer

Agreed (Q&A #12): **every cell effect applies to a layer** — Background, Crop, Zoom, Animations,
Rotate, Flip, Frames, Colors, Blur, Volume. A layer is a **floating cell**: its content (a
`SourceImage`), its `ImageLook`, and its **box** on the grid (centre, size, angle).

- **The effects toolbar acts on the selected layer** as on the selected cell: its tabs, activation
  checkboxes, options, *Reset* and the effects' own *Reset* read and write the layer's `ImageLook`.
  The selections being exclusive, the toolbar always has one target; with neither a cell nor a layer
  selected, it is disabled as today.
- An effect that does not apply to the layer's content follows the existing rule — tab selectable,
  checkbox disabled with a tooltip saying why (Frames or Volume on a still, Volume on a text).
- **Drawn by `Compositor.DrawCell`**, the layer's box standing for the cell, under the layer's
  rotation — so the preview, the exports and playback show the effects from one place.
- A **text layer** is rasterized first (§ Rendering), then drawn like a picture: its effects apply to
  that bitmap.
- The layer's **angle** (Shift + wheel) turns the whole box; the **Rotate** effect turns the content
  inside it, as in a cell. Both apply.
- **Out of scope**: the **★ Preset…** button stays a cell's — disabled while a layer is selected
  (proposed); a preset is never applied to a layer.
- **Handles on a layer**: in the **edit view** (§ Edit View), where the layer, upright, is a cell
  and its bars work as in any cell. On the grid, the Blur and the Crop show no bars on a layer.
- The **★ Preset…** button is disabled while a layer is selected, and the effects toolbar's
  **Reset** leaves the grid's separators alone then: a layer has none.
- Selecting a layer **deselects the cell** and the other way round; Ctrl+R turns the cell under the
  pointer, else the selected layer. The arrow keys move a cell's image only.
- **The Background is off by default on a layer** (Q&A #17): the content's transparency is kept — a
  cut-out PNG or a text sits on the grid with no rectangle behind it. The exception to RULES.md
  § The Background Exception for layers only: a new layer and every *Reset* bring it back off.
- **A text layer keeps its own background** setting (Q&A #15, #8), drawn into its rasterized bitmap,
  besides the Background effect of the layer.

## Rendering

- `Compositor.DrawLayers` is called by `Compositor.Draw` **after the borders**, so the layers are
  above everything, in the preview and every export (PNG, JPEG, GIF, MP4); each layer is drawn by
  `Compositor.DrawCell` into its box (`Compositor.LayerCell`), the graphics turned by its angle. The
  layers never size the canvas.
- A text layer is drawn **once per change** — text or style — into a bitmap by `Imaging/TextRaster`:
  DirectWrite through Direct2D into a WIC bitmap, the color glyphs enabled, its pixels copied into a
  GDI+ `Bitmap` at a 192 px font size (smaller past 8192 px of width), then drawn scaled like any
  picture. Its **outline** is copies of the text in the outline's color, in three rings around it, its
  emojis drawn there as plain shapes; its **box** a rectangle 15 % of a line around the text.
- The preview draws the layers into its cache: a moved, resized or turned layer, or a new frame of a
  layer's video, draws its area again — the cells under it, the borders, the layers — fast during a
  gesture, in full once it rests; a cell drawn again draws the layers over it.
- Every `Bitmap` / `Graphics` of the per-frame path released (RULES.md § Export Memory).

### Iteration 6 — 2026-10-29 — 🧭 Implementation choices

- **The color glyphs are drawn by DirectWrite through Direct2D, not WPF** (substituted variant): WPF
  draws no color emoji — its text is monochrome, the reason libraries like Emoji.Wpf exist. Direct2D's
  color fonts do, and ship with Windows: still no package, as Q&A #6 wanted. Called through their COM
  tables (`Imaging/TextRaster.cs`), which needs `AllowUnsafeBlocks` in the `.csproj`. Checked on a
  scratch render: 😀🔥👍🏽❤️ in their colors, the outline and the box.
- **Handles on a layer not delivered** (closest workable variant): the blur bars and the crop's edit
  view are written around the selected cell's index throughout `GridPreview`; giving them a layer —
  upright while edited, Q&A #14 — means reworking that whole code path. The Blur and the Crop apply to
  a layer from their options only; offered as a later adjustment.
- **The Layers tab is unavailable while the grid holds no image**: the preview draws nothing without a
  layout, so a layer would be invisible; the adding buttons, paste and drop follow.
- A paste or a drop makes **at most 10 layers** at once, each 4 % of the grid further than the previous
  one; a drop lays the first where it falls. A drop elsewhere in the window than on the grid fills the
  cells as ever.
- A new text is **empty**, its field focused; a new image layer is 40 % of the grid's shorter side, a
  new text's line 12 %.
- A text layer is drawn **once per change** at a 192 px font size and scaled, rather than cached per
  pixel size; its outline is copies of the text in rings, its emojis outlined as plain shapes.
- Over a layer, in the layer mode, the cell under it shows no hover (its buttons would not take the
  click); the cursor is a hand over a layer, the move cursor over the selected one.
- Ctrl+R turns the selected layer when the pointer is over no cell; the arrow keys move a cell's image
  only; the effects toolbar's Reset leaves the separators alone while a layer is selected.
- A layer's content from a PDF or a text file keeps the pages it was loaded with: they are not laid out
  again for its box, as a cell's are. No progress line is drawn for a layer.
- The Layers options sit on two lines beside the text field, three color options each with its
  eyedropper and Sample (RULES.md § Color Options): wider than a narrow window, where they are cut.
- Noticed, out of scope: the README documents no **Seams** global effect, though its tab exists.
