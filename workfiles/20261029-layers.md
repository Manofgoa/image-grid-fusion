# Layers

> Working document — images and texts laid over the whole grid as free **layers**, each one moved,
> resized, rotated and stacked, an emoji being a mere character of a text, drawn in color.
> This file is the source of truth for the planned work until implemented,
> then the log of every adjustment made to it afterwards.

---

## Overview

A new **global effect**, **Layers**, lays **layers** over the whole grid: an **image layer** (a
picture pasted or dropped) or a **text layer** (any text, its emojis drawn in full color). Each
layer is free on the grid — it may straddle several cells — and is moved, resized, rotated, and
raised or lowered among the others.

It supersedes `workfiles/20260927-emoji-stickers.md` (cancelled): an emoji is no longer a feature of
its own but a character of a text layer.

**Nothing changes for the cells** unless the Layers tab is selected (Q&A #5): the layers take the
mouse, the keyboard, a paste or a drop **only while that tab is selected** in the Global toolbar.
Otherwise they are drawn, and the cells behave exactly as today.

## Nature — a Global Effect

The layers belong to the **grid**, not to a cell + image pair (RULES.md § Global Effects).

| Event | Layers |
|---|---|
| An image is replaced, deleted, swapped; a cell *Reset* button | Untouched — they stay where they are on the grid |
| The layout changes, a separator is dragged, the format changes | Kept, at the same place in fractions of the grid |
| *Clear all*, the Layers options' own *Reset*, the Global tabs' *Reset* | Reset — no layer left (the initial state) |
| Its activation checkbox unchecked | Hidden from the preview and the exports, **kept**, the selected layer deselected; checked again, they come back as they were |
| Export running | The Global toolbar is locked; no layer can be selected nor edited |
| Undo / redo | Every change of the layers is a step; a drag or a wheel burst is one step; the selected layer is out of the history |

- **Off at start-up**, with no layer. Its tab holds an activation checkbox; its options show whenever
  the tab is selected; **adding a layer activates it** (acting on any option).
- **Not persisted.**
- Geometry in **fractions of the grid**: centre (x, y) in fractions of the grid's width and height,
  size in fractions of the grid's **shorter side** (the layer's longer side), angle in degrees.

## Layer Mode — the Layers Tab Selected

Agreed (Q&A #5): the layers act **only while the Layers tab is selected** in the Global toolbar, so
the layer mode never interferes with the cells.

| While the Layers tab is… | Mouse over a layer | Mouse elsewhere | Ctrl+V, a drop on the grid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selected (and the effect on, or turned on by an addition) | The layer's gestures | The cells' gestures, as today | A new layer (Q&A #6) |
| Not selected | Ignored — a click reaches the cell under it | The cells, as today | The cells, as today |

- Selecting another Global tab, or turning the effect off, **deselects** the layer.
- **Exclusive selections** (carried over, emoji stickers' Q&A #11): selecting a layer clears the
  cell selection; selecting a cell clears the layer selection.

### Gestures

Carried over from the emoji stickers (their Q&A #3, #8, #9): a layer is **selected first**.

| Gesture | On | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Click, or a drag started on it | An **unselected** layer | Selects it only — it does not move |
| Drag | The **selected** layer | Moves it |
| Wheel, pointer over it | The selected layer | Resizes it, **5 % a notch** like the resizable zones (proposed), between 3 % and 100 % of the grid's shorter side |
| *modifier* + wheel, pointer over it | The selected layer | Rotates it, 5° a notch, free angle — the modifier: open question |
| Delete | — | Removes the selected layer |
| Escape, click outside every layer | — | Deselects the layer |

- The wheel acts on the selected layer only while the pointer is over it; elsewhere it zooms the
  cell under the pointer, as today.
- Hit-testing uses the layer's **rotated rectangle**; the layer on top wins where several overlap.
- A moved layer keeps its **centre inside the grid**; the part outside is clipped at the grid edge.

### Stacking Order

Agreed (Q&A #5): the selected layer is **raised or lowered** among the others.

- **Selecting a layer no longer brings it to the front** — that would undo the order set by hand
  (revises the emoji stickers' Q&A #12).
- A new layer is added **on top**.
- The options hold **Raise** and **Lower** (one place up or down); whether **To front** / **To back**
  are added: open question.

### Selection Indicator

- The selected layer is outlined by its rotated rectangle in the **helper colour**
  (`HelperColor` / `HelperHalo`), drawn in `GridPreview.OnPaint` only — never in the exports.

## Layer Kinds

### Image Layer

- **Whatever a cell accepts** (Q&A #7): a still picture, an animated GIF, a video — its content kept
  as a `SourceImage` (`ImageLoader.FromImage` for a pasted bitmap, the file loader for a file),
  never put in a cell.
- **Added two ways** (Q&A #6):
  - while the Layers tab is selected, **Ctrl+V** and a **drop on the grid** (a file, an explorer
    tile) create a layer instead of filling a cell — the check at the top of `MainForm.Paste()`,
    `OnDragDrop`, `OnDragEnter`, `OnPreviewDragOver`, no cell drop-target highlight meanwhile; with
    any other tab, both behave exactly as today;
  - **Add image…** (a file picker) and **Paste** buttons in the Layers options.
  - A pasted text, while the Layers tab is selected, makes a **text layer**.
  - The ✥ handle's swap is untouched: it moves cells, never makes a layer.
- Its aspect ratio is kept; its size is its longer side.
- **An animated layer plays on the grid's clock** (RULES.md § Transport): from its first frame at
  the clock's origin, looping on its whole length — no trim, no starting point (proposed). Its loop
  **counts in the video length** (`Animation.VideoLength` / `Animation.LoopOf` take the layers'
  loops); the preview (`AnimationPlayer`) and the export's frames (`GridExport`) both read it there.
- **The Cascade** leaves the layers out: they play alongside, like the contents taking no turn
  (proposed).
- **Its sound**: a video layer's sound joins the grid's mix (preview `PreviewSound`, export
  `MixedSound`), under the Fade like the rest — its own control: open question.
- An image layer leaving the grid (deleted, reset, undone) goes through `GridPreview.ReleaseImage`
  like a cell's, so the undo history keeps it alive and counts it in its parked bytes.

### Text Layer

- Any text, several lines allowed; its emojis drawn in **full color**, nothing emoji-specific in the
  app (the user's 2026-10-10 request).
- **Typed in the options** (Q&A #9): an **Add text** button creates a text layer — on top, at the
  grid's centre — selected, its **multi-line field** in the Layers options focused; the field edits
  the selected text layer, live; Win+. opens the Windows emoji panel. An empty text is drawn as
  nothing and stays selectable by its frame while selected.
- **Its settings** (Q&A #8), shown in the options for the selected text layer, disabled for an image
  layer or none:

  | Setting | Default (proposed) |
  |---|---|
  | **Font** (installed families) and **Bold** | Segoe UI, bold |
  | **Color** — the color split button, eyedropper and Color… (RULES.md § Color Options); the emojis keep their own colors | White |
  | **Outline** — on / off, its color (split button) and thickness | On, black, 8 % of the text height |
  | **Background** — on / off, its color (split button) and opacity, a box behind the text with a margin | Off, black, 50 % |
- Its size is its **text height** (a line's), in fractions of the grid's shorter side; the wheel
  scales it like any layer.

## Rendering

- `GridLayers.Draw(Graphics, Size canvas)` is called by `Compositor.Draw` **after the borders**, so
  the layers are above everything, in the preview and every export (PNG, JPEG, GIF, MP4).
- A text layer is **rasterized by WPF** (`FormattedText` into a `RenderTargetBitmap`, its pixels
  copied into a GDI+ `Bitmap`), **cached** by (text, settings, pixel size), then drawn with
  `g.DrawImage` under a rotation transform, like an image layer; the cache keeps a video export from
  rasterizing every frame. WPF is used for this rasterization only; the UI stays WinForms.
- Every `Bitmap` / `Graphics` of the per-frame path released (RULES.md § Export Memory).

## Design Iterations

Chronological log of design refinements, of the choices the implementation run
took on its own — flagged `🧭 Implementation choices` — and of every adjustment
requested afterwards — flagged `⚙️ Post-implementation`. One entry per request,
in the order the requests were made.

### Iteration 1 — 2026-10-29

- Topic: images and texts as free layers over the whole grid, emojis being characters of a text
  drawn in color — superseding the cancelled emoji stickers (`20260927-emoji-stickers.md`) and the
  text overlay requested on 2026-10-10, never designed.
- Scoping batch not answered as asked: the user replied that the layer mode must never interfere
  with the cells — a Layers tab in the Global toolbar, the layers selectable only while it is
  selected — and that the selected layer must be raised or lowered. The depth question unanswered,
  the agent kept a single scout pass.
- Carried over from the emoji stickers' design: a global effect, select first, drag / wheel over the
  layer, Delete, Escape, exclusive selections, WPF for the color glyphs. Their "selected comes to the
  front" is dropped for the explicit Raise / Lower.
- Scout pass (three read-only agents): the paste and drop routes and their interception point; the
  global effect wiring as it stands (`GlobalState`, `CaptureState`, the Global toolbar); the
  preview's input chains, the missing `LayersActive` flag, and no color emoji anywhere today.
- Proposed defaults, to be confirmed with the design: wheel 5 % a notch, size 3 %–100 % of the
  shorter side, 5° a notch, a new layer on top, layers drawn above the borders.

### Iteration 2 — 2026-10-29

First batch answered: image layers added both by paste / drop while the Layers tab is selected and by
buttons in the options; GIFs and videos may be layers, sound included; a text layer has a font and
bold, a color, an outline and a background; its text typed in a field of the options. The animated
layers bring their own design — the grid's clock, the video length, the mix, the Cascade left out —
and a new open question on their sound.
