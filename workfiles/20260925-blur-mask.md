# Blur Mask

> Working document — a per-cell "Effects" entry point, whose first effect blurs a rectangle of the
> cell delimited by four draggable bars.
> This file is the source of truth for the planned work until implemented,
> then the log of every adjustment made to it afterwards.

---

## Overview

Add an always-visible **effects toolbar** at the top of the window, reflecting the effects of the
selected image. Selecting an effect opens a second toolbar, below the first, holding that effect's
options. Clicking the selected effect again removes it.

The first effect is a **bar blur**: two horizontal and two vertical bars delimit a rectangle of
the cell. Each side moves on its own (top / right / bottom / left), so the user keeps exactly the
rectangle to blur. A bar dragged close to the cell edge **snaps fully to it**, so no 1–2 px unblurred
strip is left along that edge.

Agreed at scoping (Q&A #1–#3):

- The effect is **per cell**: each cell carries its own effects; the rectangle lives in that cell.
- The blur options are an **intensity** and a **type** (gaussian blur or pixelate).
- The blur shows in the **on-screen preview**, in the **image export**, and on **videos during
  playback**, in real time. It is **not persisted** — the app keeps no session, and this effect
  adds none.

## UI — Effects Toolbar and Options Toolbar

- **No Effects button, nothing in the cell** (Q&A #5, #6): the effects toolbar lives in the main
  window's top area and is **always visible**. It reflects the effects of the **selected image**
  (`GridPreview._selected`): each effect is a toggle button, **pressed** when that effect is active
  on the selected cell. The cell's hover toolbar is unchanged.
- **Effects toolbar**: one toggle button per available effect (only *Blur* for now), on a **new
  row below the existing top bar** (threshold slider, carousel checkbox) (Q&A #18).
- **Click semantics** (Q&A #7):

  | Effect state on the selected image | Click does |
  |---|---|
  | Inactive | Activates it with its defaults and selects it (options shown) |
  | Active, not selected | Selects it (options shown) |
  | Active and selected | Deactivates it (options hidden) |

- **Options toolbar**: a row shown below the effects row while an effect is selected. For the
  blur: a type switch (*Gaussian* / *Pixelate*) and an intensity slider.
- **Effects belong to the cell + image pair** (Q&A #17): when the image of a cell changes, the
  cell's effects go back to their defaults (none active). Which events count as "the image
  changes" — *open (Q&A #19).*
- **Selection changes**: what happens to the selected effect when **another cell** is selected,
  and the toolbar's state when no cell is selected — *open (Q&A #20).*
- **Reset**: the cell's *Reset* tool also removes its effects, and is shown whenever an effect is
  active, even with no other action to undo (Q&A #13).

## Blur Effect — Bars and Snapping

- **Model**: the rectangle is stored as four fractions `Left < Right`, `Top < Bottom`, in `[0, 1]`
  of the **cell** (Q&A #8): zooming, panning or rotating the image does not move it. Stored with the effect's type and intensity on
  the cell's immutable state (an `Effects` part next to `ImageLook`, or inside it — implementation
  choice). Defaults on activation: **gaussian**, intensity at **mid-course** of the slider
  (Q&A #12).
- **Bars**: two vertical (left, right) and two horizontal (top, bottom), drawn on the **selected
  cell** only while the blur is the selected effect in the effects toolbar. Style: **full-length
  guides** crossing the whole cell, grabbable along their whole length, with a small grip at the
  middle of the rectangle's side (Q&A #16).
- **Drag**: each bar moves on its own axis only; the cursor is `SizeWE` / `SizeNS` over a bar. A
  bar never crosses its opposite one (a minimum gap is kept). Only the bars move: a drag inside the
  rectangle keeps the cell's current gestures (Q&A #11).
- **Snap**: while dragging, a bar within **6 px** of its cell edge lands **exactly** on it (fraction
  `0` or `1`), so the blur reaches the edge with no leftover strip (Q&A #10). Logical pixels on
  screen, scaled with `LogicalToDeviceUnits`.
- **Default rectangle** on activation: **centered, half the cell's width and height** — fractions
  `0.25 … 0.75` on both axes (Q&A #9).
- **Priority over existing gestures**: a press on a bar starts a bar drag before any pan / swap
  hit-test; elsewhere, the cell keeps its current gestures.

## Rendering

- **Hook**: `Compositor.DrawCell`, after the frame is drawn and still under the cell clip — so the
  preview, the still export, the animated export and live video frames all get the blur from one
  place.
- **Technique** (GDI+, no per-pixel loop, cheap enough for video):
  - draw the blurred part of the cell into a small offscreen bitmap (cell size ÷ factor), then draw
    it back stretched, clipped to the blur rectangle;
  - *Gaussian*: downscale with high-quality interpolation, upscale bilinear (two-step downscale
    when the factor is large, to avoid blockiness);
  - *Pixelate*: upscale with nearest-neighbour — the factor is the block size.
- **Resolution independence**: the intensity is expressed relative to the cell size, not in
  pixels, so the preview and an export at another size look the same.
- **Order**: the blur applies to the image as shown (after rotation, flips, zoom and grayscale),
  bands included when the rectangle covers them.

## Design Iterations

Chronological log of design refinements, of the choices the implementation run
took on its own — flagged `🧭 Implementation choices` — and of every adjustment
requested afterwards — flagged `⚙️ Post-implementation`. One entry per request,
in the order the requests were made.

### Iteration 1 — 2026-09-25

Initial design from the request and the scoping batch (Q&A #1–#4: per cell, intensity + type,
preview + export + live video, not persisted, straightforward subject → single scout pass).

Scout pass (three read-only agents): toolbars are plain WinForms panels in `MainForm` plus
owner-drawn per-cell tools in `GridPreview`; per-cell state is the immutable `ImageLook`;
`Compositor.DrawCell` is the single render choke point shared by preview, exports and video
playback; no test project exists. The pending `20260924-move-image-icon.md` workfile (not
implemented) changes the pan gesture — the bar drag takes priority over it either way.

Proposed: an owner-drawn effects row and options row, a four-fraction rectangle on the cell state,
a GDI+ downscale/upscale blur in `DrawCell`, and twelve open questions (Q&A #5–#16).

### Iteration 2 — 2026-09-25

The user dropped the *Effects* button of the initial request and anything drawn in the cell for
the toolbars (Q&A #5, #6): the effects toolbar is always visible at the top of the window and
reflects the selected image, its toggles pressed for the active effects. Click semantics confirmed
(Q&A #7); the rectangle is anchored to the cell (Q&A #8). The bars stay in the cell, on the
selected image, while the blur is selected. Two questions emerge from the new placement
(Q&A #17, #18).

### Iteration 3 — 2026-09-25

Bar behaviour and defaults settled (Q&A #9–#12): centered half-cell rectangle on activation, 6 px
snap to the cell edge, bars only (no whole-rectangle move), gaussian at medium intensity.

### Iteration 4 — 2026-09-25

Effects row on a new line below the top bar (Q&A #18), full-length guides (Q&A #16), *Reset* also
removes the effects (Q&A #13). Q&A #17 was read as "the image of the cell changes": effects belong
to the cell + image pair and reset when the image changes — a new rule, whose triggering events are
asked as Q&A #19. The intended question (another cell selected) is asked again as Q&A #20.
