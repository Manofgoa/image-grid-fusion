# Zoom Fit

> Working document — two persistent fitting modes in the Zoom tab: the image contained whole in its
> cell, or filling it.
> This file is the source of truth for the planned work until implemented,
> then the log of every adjustment made to it afterwards.

---

## Overview

The Zoom effect's value is a **scale of the fitting rule** (`ImageLook.Zoom`, 1 = the image as the
fitting rule draws it: filling the cell, cropping at most 15 % — `FitCalculator.CropThreshold` —
then leaving bands). Today the only way to show a whole image, or to make one fill its cell past the
threshold, is to zoom by hand, and the value is lost as soon as the cell changes size.

This adds two **fit modes** to the Zoom tab's options:

- **Contain** — the image whole inside its cell, touching it on one axis, bands on the other.
- **Fill** — the image covering the whole cell, no band, the overflow cropped.

A mode is **persistent**: while it is on, the zoom is **computed** from the cell and the image, so it
stays fitted when the cell changes size (separator, layout, output format, Borders' gap), when the
image is cropped or turned a quarter turn, or when it is swapped into another cell.

Components: `Composition/ImageLook.cs` (the mode, part of the Zoom effect's state),
`Composition/FitCalculator.cs` (the zoom a mode gives), `Composition/Compositor.cs` and
`UI/GridPreview.cs` (every reader of the zoom), `UI/MainForm.cs` (the Zoom tab's options).

## Behaviour

### The Modes

| Mode | Zoom computed | Result |
|---|---|---|
| None (default) | `ImageLook.Zoom`, as today | Free zoom |
| Contain | `min(cell.W / image.W, cell.H / image.H) / FitCalculator.Scale(…)` | The whole image in the cell; bands on one axis (none when the ratios match) |
| Fill | `max(cell.W / image.W, cell.H / image.H) / FitCalculator.Scale(…)` | The cell covered; the overflow cropped |

- `image` is **`ImageLook.Shown`** — cropped, then rotated — as the Crop Exception requires of every
  reader of the image's size (RULES.md § The Crop Exception).
- The cell is the drawn cell (`Compositor.Cells`, the Borders' gap applied), the same rectangle
  `ComputeTurned` already receives.
- Fill gives 100 % whenever the fitting rule already fills the cell (overflow ≤ 15 %); Contain gives
  100 % or less. Both are relative values: the zoom shown is the computed one.
- The mode is **independent of the position**: turning a mode on keeps the focus (the image stays
  where it was moved), then brings it back within its stops, as every zoom change does
  (`FitCalculator.WithinStops`). **Dragging** the image while a mode is on moves it and **keeps the
  mode**.

### Leaving a Mode

| Action while a mode is on | Result |
|---|---|
| The wheel over the cell, the slider (drag, keys, its own wheel) | The mode is **left**: the zoom starts from the computed value and is free from then on; the mode's button unchecks |
| The checked mode button clicked (unchecked) | The mode is left, the zoom **kept at the computed value**, free from then on |
| The other mode's button clicked | Switches to that mode |
| Dragging the image | The mode is kept, only the focus changes |

Leaving a mode writes the computed value into `ImageLook.Zoom` — for the cell it was computed in — so
nothing jumps.

### State

- The mode is part of the **Zoom effect's state** on `ImageLook`, next to `Zoom` and `Focus`:
  immutable, not persisted.
- Turning the Zoom effect **off** keeps the mode with the kept zoom (`KeptZoom`), brought back on
  `TurnOn`; off, the image is drawn at the defaults (100 %, centered), as today.
- The Zoom effect's **Reset**, the toolbar's **Reset**, a replaced image and an image shifting after a
  deletion bring the mode back to **None** (default state, RULES.md § Scope and State).
- A **swap** keeps it: the image arrives in its new cell fitted to it.
- **Undo**: the mode is part of the `ImageLook` captured by a step; `ChangedEffects` counts a mode
  change as a Zoom change, and a restore whose effective zoom changes shows the zoom badge.
- Turning a mode on is acting on an option: it **turns the Zoom effect on** (RULES.md § Options
  Toolbar).

### One Reader of the Zoom

Every place that draws or measures the image reads the **effective zoom** — `ImageLook.ZoomIn(Rectangle
cell, Size shown)`, resolving the mode against the cell and the shown size through
`FitCalculator.FitZoom`, clamped to [`MinZoom`, `MaxZoom`], and its time-dependent sibling
`ImageLook.ZoomAt(time, cell, shown)` for the Animations effect — never `ImageLook.Zoom` directly
(RULES.md § Rendering):

- `Compositor.DrawCell` (both `ComputeTurned` calls, through `ZoomAt`) — the preview, the exports and
  video playback alike (RULES.md § Rendering);
- `GridPreview`: placement, hit-testing and dragging, the zoom badge, the restored indicators, the
  wheel's starting value, `FrameDisplaySize` (the decoding size of animated frames);
- `MainForm`: the slider and the `Zoom: N %` label show the **effective** zoom of the selected cell
  (`GridPreview.SelectedZoom`), refreshed when the cell changes shape (layout, separators, format and
  Borders all end in `UpdateButtons` → `UpdateEffects`).

While a mode is on, `ImageLook.Zoom` keeps its last free value, unread. Leaving the mode
(`GridPreview.FitSelected(ZoomFit.None)`, the wheel, the slider) writes the computed value into it.
Turning a mode on or off shows the zoom badge.

The Animations effect multiplies the effective zoom, as it multiplies the free one today. The fine
angle keeps its rule on top of the effective zoom: the mode fits the **unturned** image, then the
fine angle zooms it just enough to keep covering what it covers unturned — with Contain, a turned
image is therefore no longer entirely visible. `FitCalculator.ComputeTurned` is unchanged.

`OutputFormats.FreeRatio` and `CanvasSizer` do not read the zoom today and keep not reading it: the
Free format is computed on the fitting rule, then the mode fits the image to the cells it gives —
no feedback loop.

### UI — Zoom Tab

- After the slider and its label: two **option toggle buttons** (`OptionButton`, as Crop's ratios and
  Flip's buttons), **Contain** and **Fill**, mutually exclusive — at most one checked.
- Checked = that mode on. Clicking the unchecked one switches to it; unchecking, the wheel and the
  slider: § Leaving a Mode.
- While a mode is on, the slider stays enabled and shows the computed zoom.
- The Zoom tab's icon and its activation checkbox are unchanged.

## Design Iterations

### Iteration 1 — 2026-10-07

Initial design from the scoping answers: two persistent modes, Contain and Fill, stored on the Zoom
effect's state; the zoom they give computed from the cell and `ImageLook.Shown` through one
effective-zoom reader used by `Compositor`, `GridPreview` and `MainForm`; two toggle buttons in the
Zoom tab; the position kept. Five interaction questions left open.

### Iteration 2 — 2026-10-07

Interaction answers: the wheel and the slider leave the mode (free zoom from the computed value);
dragging keeps it; unchecking keeps the computed value; labels Contain / Fill; the fine angle's
covering zoom applies on top of the mode, `ComputeTurned` unchanged. New § Leaving a Mode. No open
question left.

### Iteration 3 — 2026-10-07 — ✅ Implemented

Go given: code, tests and documentation. Branch Gate: **stays on `main`**, the repository's standing
choice (work lands on `main`).

### Iteration 4 — 2026-10-07 — 🧭 Implementation choices

No rule broken. Choices the design left open:

- **Names**: enum `ZoomFit { None, Contain, Fill }` (in `ImageLook.cs`), property `ImageLook.ZoomFit`,
  `ImageLook.ZoomIn` / `ImageLook.WithZoomFit`, `FitCalculator.FitZoom`, `GridPreview.FitSelected` /
  `GridPreview.SelectedZoom`. `ImageLook.ZoomAt(time)` became `ZoomAt(time, cell, shown)`.
- The kept zoom (`KeptZoom`) carries the mode too, so turning the Zoom effect off then on brings the
  mode back; a quarter turn or a flip turns the kept focus as before, the mode kept.
- The computed zoom is **clamped** to [`MinZoom`, `MaxZoom`], like a free zoom.
- `ImageLook.Zoom` is left as it was while a mode is on (unread); `WithZoom` sets the mode back to
  None, so every free zoom route — the wheel, the slider, its wheel — leaves the mode by itself.
- Turning a mode on or off shows the **zoom badge** with the zoom it gives.
- A restored step shows the zoom badge when the **effective** zoom changes (`ShowRestored`), a mode
  change counting as a Zoom change in `ChangedEffects`.
- Tooltips: *"Keeps the whole image in its cell, whatever its size: bands on one side"* and *"Keeps
  the cell covered by the image, whatever its size: the overflow cropped"*.
- Documentation beyond the README: the glossary gets **Fit mode** (both languages) and RULES.md
  § Rendering the rule that the zoom is read through `ImageLook.ZoomIn`.
