# Toolbar

> Working document — move the cell's hover tools into the effects toolbar as effects, and turn the
> global crop threshold into a per-cell effect.
> This file is the source of truth for the planned work until implemented,
> then the log of every adjustment made to it afterwards.

---

## Overview

The blur mask (`workfiles/20260925-blur-mask.md`, delivered) introduced the **effects toolbar**,
the **options toolbar** and the **selected cell** (see `RULES.md` § Effects). This workfile builds
on that framework:

- The actions currently drawn on a hovered cell become **effects** of the effects toolbar, acting
  on the **selected cell**, with their settings in the options toolbar.
- Some top-bar settings that act on the whole grid become per-cell effects — first of all the
  **Crop** threshold slider (top left), which today applies to every image.
- The effects row gets a leading **"Effects"** label.

Every moved feature follows `RULES.md` § Effects (state on the cell + image pair, click cycle,
options row, handles on the selected cell only, rendering in `Compositor.DrawCell`).

## Effects Toolbar

- A leading **"Effects"** label, at the start of the row, before the first button. It is a plain
  label: never pressed, never disabled-looking beyond the row's own state.
- The buttons follow `RULES.md` § Effects Toolbar (pressed = active on the selected cell; disabled
  with no cell selected; click cycle inactive → active + selected → deselected… → deactivated).
- Order — geometry first, then rendering: **Effects** label · Zoom · Rotate · Flip · Frames ·
  Black & white · Blur · then the **Reset** button.
- **Deactivating** an effect restores its default — Zoom 100 % centered, Rotate 0°, Flip none,
  Frames from the beginning and playing, Black & white off, Blur off; activating it again starts
  from its defaults.
- Consequence of moving the tools: every moved action now needs a **selected cell**. Hovering a
  cell no longer shows its rotate / flip / B&W / reset buttons.
- An effect that does not apply to the selected image has its button **disabled** (Frames on a
  still image).
- Every action on the image being an effect, an image that moves into another cell after a deletion
  loses them all — rotation, flips and zoom included (`ImageLook.WithoutEffects` is now `None`).

## Effect: Rotate

- Options row: the **4 rotations** (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°), as mutually exclusive buttons, plus a
  **fine-angle slider**, by **1°** steps from **−45° to +45°**, added to the chosen quarter turn.
- The rotation button is **pressed when the angle falls exactly on it** (fine offset 0°); with any
  other offset, no rotation button is pressed. Clicking a rotation button sets that angle and
  brings the slider back to 0°.
- At a non-right angle, the image turns around the **center of the cell**, **zoomed just enough to
  keep covering the part of the cell it covers unturned** (`FitCalculator.Turn`): an image that
  fills its cell keeps filling it, as with a photo editor's *Straighten* tool; one that leaves
  bands (zoomed out, portrait in a landscape cell) keeps them. When the image is moved so far that
  the cell's center lies beyond one of its sides, that side cannot be reached by scaling and is left
  out; the scale is capped at 8.
- **Against the pan beyond the cell's edges** (`workfiles/20260925-pan-beyond-cell-bounds.md`,
  § Fine Rotation, Q&A #11 there — Iteration 9 here):
  - on a turned image, the **magnetic stops, the 10 % margin and the guides** are computed on the
    **axis-aligned bounding box** of the turned image;
  - the **cover zoom applies only while the image is within its stops**; once pushed beyond, its
    position is kept as it is, the zoom no longer grows, and the uncovered corners get the band
    color.
- Dragging and the wheel follow the mouse on a turned image.
- Replaces the rotate-left / rotate-right hover buttons.

## Effect: Flip

- One **Flip** effect; its options hold two toggles, **Horizontal** and **Vertical**.
- Replaces the two flip hover toggles.

## Effect: Black & White

- Options: an **intensity** slider, desaturating from 0 to 100 % (100 % when activated); the bands
  follow it.
- Replaces the black & white hover toggle.

## Background Color

- The band color keeps today's rule (`README.md` § Fitting rules): the image's own background when
  at least three sides carry one uniform color, otherwise the most frequent color of the image.
- It is **recomputed from the part the cell actually shows** once every effect is applied — fine
  rotation and per-cell crop included, as it already follows zoom and focus.

## Effect: Zoom

- Options: the **zoom slider**, 50 % to 400 % on a log scale (hundredths of a doubling), snapping to
  100 % as today, and its percentage; replaces the slider along the cell's left edge.
- The **mouse wheel** and the **drag to move** keep working on the cell under the mouse, as today —
  selected or not. The effect is active as soon as the image is zoomed or moved.

## Effect: Frames

- Replaces the frames / pages slider drawn along the bottom of a hovered animated cell (video,
  animated GIF, multi-page PDF, long text — the same principle for each).
- Options: the frames / pages **slider**, its label (*Starts at: …* / *Frozen on: …*, with the
  source's own page label or time), and a **Freeze** checkbox.
- The position is stored as a **share of the frames** (0 = first, 1 = last), not as a page, so it
  stays about in place when a text is laid out again on more or fewer pages.
- **Not frozen**: the slider sets the **starting point** of the playback — the **beginning** of the
  content by default — in the preview and in the exported video, the exported **sound** starting at
  the same point.
- **Frozen**: the cell stops playing, and the slider picks the frame (or page) it shows. The exports
  use that frame as a **still**, rendered from the source at full size; when every cell is frozen
  or still, Copy / Save produce a PNG, and *Force as image* is hidden. A frozen video has no sound:
  the next video with sound takes over.
- Hovering a cell **no longer holds it still**: it keeps playing; freezing goes through the
  checkbox only.

## Reset

- A **Reset** button at the end of the effects toolbar — not an effect, never pressed. It removes
  every effect of the selected cell, as the cell's hover *Reset* tool did.

## Stays on the Cell

- **×** (remove, plus the `Delete` key) and the **✥** swap handle.
- The mouse wheel zoom and the drag to move (see *Effect: Zoom*).

## Crop Threshold

- The global **Crop slider and its label are removed** from the top bar: once the image can be
  zoomed out and panned past the cell's edges (`workfiles/20260925-pan-beyond-cell-bounds.md`,
  designed in another session), the zoom and the position do its job.
- The fitting rule keeps a **fixed 15 %** threshold — today's default — so an untouched image looks
  as it does today. `CanvasSizer` and the exports use the same constant (`FitCalculator.CropThreshold`);
  the threshold parameters are gone from every signature.
- **Before the implementation starts**, this decision is sent to the *Image panning outside cell
  bounds* session, whose design touches the same `FitCalculator` code (user request, Q&A #12).

### Iteration 8 — 2026-09-25 — 🧭 Implementation choices

No project rule was broken. Choices the frozen design did not state:

- **Branch**: stayed on `main`, the standing choice for this app (memory *work-on-main-only*), no
  question asked.
- **Effects model**: `ImageEffect` enum in toolbar order; Zoom, Rotate and Flip carry an
  *activated* flag, so they are active (button pressed) even at their default values; Frames,
  Black & white and Blur are active while their settings are set. The wheel and the drag activate
  Zoom on any cell.
- **Images shifting cells**: `WithoutEffects` now returns `None` — rotation, flips and zoom are
  effects, so a deletion resets them on the images that move into another cell (they used to be
  kept as "actions").
- **Cover zoom**: covers the part of the cell the image covered unturned, around the cell's center;
  a banded image keeps its bands; scale capped at 8 when the cell's center lies beyond the image.
  Gestures on a turned image follow the mouse. Not taken into account: the canvas size (as for the
  zoom) and the decoding size of video frames.
- **Frames**: position stored as a share of the frames; labels *Starts at* / *Frozen on*; frozen
  pages rendered at full size in the preview (page loader) and in the exports; the exported sound
  starts at the video's starting point (`VideoEncoder` gets a sound start, samples before it are
  dropped); a frozen video gives up the sound; *Force as image* only shows while content plays.
- **During an export**: the effects row is disabled (as the blur was); the README no longer says
  the sliders keep working.
- **Top bar**: only the *Carrousel* checkbox is left in it.
- **Icons**: Zoom, Rotate, Flip, Frames, Black & white and Reset icons drawn in `EffectIcons`; the
  Black & white intensity reuses the Blur intensity wedge.
- **Documentation**: besides the README, `RULES.md` (effects row, disabled effects, Reset,
  deactivation restores the defaults) and `GLOSSARY.md` (effects list, fine angle, starting point,
  frozen) were updated.
- **Verification**: builds after every commit; a scratch render of four fine angles checked the
  turn direction and the cover; the window was captured once (label, buttons, Reset, no Crop
  slider). Video, GIF, PDF and text contents, and the exports, were not exercised: no test media.

### Iteration 9 — 2026-09-25 — ⚙️ Post-implementation — Fine angle and pan beyond the cell's edges

- Decision taken by the user in the *Image panning outside cell bounds* session (its workfile,
  § Fine Rotation, Q&A #11), to be applied by this workfile: on a turned image, the magnetic stops,
  the 10 % margin and the guides use the **axis-aligned bounding box** of the turned image; the
  **cover zoom applies only within the stops** — pushed beyond, the image keeps its position and its
  uncovered corners get the band color. Replaces the stops of the unturned image (Iteration 8).
- User go in this session: "Oui, maintenant".
