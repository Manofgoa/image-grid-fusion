# Toolbar

> Working document — move the cell's hover tools into the effects toolbar as effects, and turn the
> global crop threshold into a per-cell effect.
> This file is the source of truth for the planned work until implemented,
> then the log of every adjustment made to it afterwards.

---

## Overview

The blur mask (`workfiles/20260925-blur-mask.md`, delivered) introduced the **effects toolbar**,
the **options toolbar** and the **selected cell** (see `RULES.md` § Effects). This workfile builds
on that framework:

- The actions currently drawn on a hovered cell become **effects** of the effects toolbar, acting
  on the **selected cell**, with their settings in the options toolbar.
- Some top-bar settings that act on the whole grid become per-cell effects — first of all the
  **Crop** threshold slider (top left), which today applies to every image.
- The effects row gets a leading **"Effects"** label.

Every moved feature follows `RULES.md` § Effects (state on the cell + image pair, click cycle,
options row, handles on the selected cell only, rendering in `Compositor.DrawCell`).

## Effects Toolbar

- A leading **"Effects"** label, at the start of the row, before the first button. It is a plain
  label: never pressed, never disabled-looking beyond the row's own state.
- The buttons follow `RULES.md` § Effects Toolbar (pressed = active on the selected cell; disabled
  with no cell selected; click cycle inactive → active + selected → deselected… → deactivated).
- Order — geometry first, then rendering: **Effects** label · Zoom · Rotate · Flip · Frames ·
  Black & white · Blur · then the **Reset** button.
- **Deactivating** an effect restores its default — Zoom 100 % centered, Rotate 0°, Flip none,
  Frames from the beginning and playing, Black & white off, Blur off; activating it again starts
  from its defaults.
- Consequence of moving the tools: every moved action now needs a **selected cell**. Hovering a
  cell no longer shows its rotate / flip / B&W / reset buttons.

## Effect: Rotate

- Options row: the **4 rotations** (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°), as mutually exclusive buttons, plus a
  **fine-angle slider**, by **1°** steps from **−45° to +45°**, added to the chosen quarter turn.
- The rotation button is **pressed when the angle falls exactly on it** (fine offset 0°); with any
  other offset, no rotation button is pressed. Clicking a rotation button sets that angle and
  brings the slider back to 0°.
- At a non-right angle, the image is **zoomed just enough to cover the cell**: no corner of the
  cell is left uncovered, as with a photo editor's *Straighten* tool.
- Replaces the rotate-left / rotate-right hover buttons.

## Effect: Flip

- One **Flip** effect; its options hold two toggles, **Horizontal** and **Vertical**.
- Replaces the two flip hover toggles.

## Effect: Black & White

- Options: an **intensity** slider, desaturating from 0 to 100 %.
- Replaces the black & white hover toggle.

## Background Color

- The band color keeps today's rule (`README.md` § Fitting rules): the image's own background when
  at least three sides carry one uniform color, otherwise the most frequent color of the image.
- It is **recomputed from the part the cell actually shows** once every effect is applied — fine
  rotation and per-cell crop included, as it already follows zoom and focus.

## Effect: Zoom

- Options: the **zoom slider**, 50 % to 400 %, snapping to 100 % as today; replaces the slider along
  the cell's left edge.
- The **mouse wheel** and the **drag to move** keep working on the cell under the mouse, as today —
  selected or not. The effect is active as soon as the image is zoomed or moved.

## Effect: Frames

- Replaces the frames / pages slider drawn along the bottom of a hovered animated cell (video,
  animated GIF, multi-page PDF, long text — the same principle for each).
- Options: the frames / pages **slider**, and a **Freeze** checkbox.
- **Not frozen**: the slider sets the **starting point** of the playback — the **beginning** of the
  content by default — in the preview and in the exported video.
- **Frozen**: the cell stops playing, and the slider picks the frame (or page) it shows. The exports
  use that frame as a **still**; when every cell is frozen or still, Copy / Save produce a PNG.
- Hovering a cell **no longer holds it still**: it keeps playing; freezing goes through the
  checkbox only.

## Reset

- A **Reset** button at the end of the effects toolbar — not an effect, never pressed. It removes
  every effect of the selected cell, as the cell's hover *Reset* tool did.

## Stays on the Cell

- **×** (remove, plus the `Delete` key) and the **✥** swap handle.
- The mouse wheel zoom and the drag to move (see *Effect: Zoom*).

## Crop Threshold

- The global **Crop slider and its label are removed** from the top bar: once the image can be
  zoomed out and panned past the cell's edges (`workfiles/20260925-pan-beyond-cell-bounds.md`,
  designed in another session), the zoom and the position do its job.
- The fitting rule keeps a **fixed 15 %** threshold — today's default — so an untouched image looks
  as it does today. `CanvasSizer` and the exports use the same constant.
- **Before the implementation starts**, this decision is sent to the *Image panning outside cell
  bounds* session, whose design touches the same `FitCalculator` code (user request, Q&A #12).

## Test Impact

No unit test is created or updated: the user chose to keep the solution test-free (Q&A #15), as
every previous workfile did. The fine angle and its cover zoom, the Frames starting point and
frozen still, and the fixed 15 % threshold stay untested by decision, not because nothing testable
changes. Verification is manual: each effect on a photo and on a video cell, the toolbar with no
cell selected, Reset, then a copy / save (PNG and MP4).

## Design Iterations

Chronological log of design refinements, of the choices the implementation run
took on its own — flagged `🧭 Implementation choices` — and of every adjustment
requested afterwards — flagged `⚙️ Post-implementation`. One entry per request,
in the order the requests were made.

### Iteration 1 — 2026-09-25

- Scoping answers: moved features act on the **selected cell**; the moved cell tools **are
  effects** (e.g. Rotate with the 4 rotations as options plus a fine-angle slider); full inventory
  from the code, one decision per row; subject judged straightforward (single scout pass).
- User request during exploration: a leading **"Effects"** label on the effects toolbar.
- Inventory written (cell hover tools, top bar, bottom bar, layout strip, drop zone), with a
  proposed destination per row; the open points listed in `## Open Questions`.

### Iteration 2 — 2026-09-25

- OQ 1–4 answered: Rotate gets a 1° fine-angle slider (−45° to +45°) with the matching rotation
  button pressed, and an auto zoom covering the cell; one Flip effect with H / V options; Black &
  white gets an intensity slider. Domain sections added for Rotate, Flip and Black & White.

### Iteration 3 — 2026-09-25

- User request: the band color is recomputed with the same rules as before (uniform sides, else
  dominant color), from the part the cell shows once the effects apply. Section *Background
  Color* added.

### Iteration 4 — 2026-09-25

- OQ 5–7 answered: Zoom becomes an effect (slider in the options), the wheel and the drag to move
  stay on the cell; Reset becomes a button at the end of the effects toolbar; × and ✥ stay on the
  cell; the frames / pages slider becomes the **Frames** effect, with a **Freeze** checkbox.
- OQ 8 not settled: the user expects the Crop slider to become useless with the pan beyond the
  cell's edges (other session), and asks that this be sent to that session before implementing.
  The per-cell *Crop* effect section is withdrawn; OQ 8 reworded, OQ 12–14 added for Frames.

### Iteration 5 — 2026-09-25

- OQ 8, 12–14 answered: the global Crop slider is removed, the fitting rule keeping a fixed 15 %
  threshold; Frames — not frozen, the slider sets the starting point; frozen, the chosen frame is
  exported as a still (all frozen → PNG); hovering no longer holds a cell still.
- The Crop decision is sent to the *Image panning outside cell bounds* session (Q&A #12).

### Iteration 6 — 2026-09-25

- OQ 9–11 answered: order geometry then rendering; deactivating an effect restores its default;
  no unit tests. Every open question resolved — design submitted for the go.
