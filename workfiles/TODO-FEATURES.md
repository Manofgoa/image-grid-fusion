| **Preset dropped onto the Layers keeps its effects** — a preset tile dropped into a cell arrives with its effects (`MainForm.OnDragDrop` passes `FileExplorerPanel.PresetLookOf` to `AddFilesAsync`), but dropped onto the Layers tab or its options it gets `Layer.DefaultLook`: `DropLayerFilesAsync` → `AddLayerFilesAsync` → `AddImageLayers` carry no look. Carry the preset's look down to the new layer; its Background stays off unless the preset has a `background` key (`LookPreset.FromJson` starts from `ImageLook.None`, Background on). The Cutout already works on a layer, so an image cut out and saved as a preset becomes a sticker. | **Emoji Kitchen layers** — two emojis combined into one sticker by Google's Emoji Kitchen (🐱 + 🌵), laid as a layer. | **Strip AI metadata** — remove the marks an AI generator writes into its images (Grok and others): **C2PA** content credentials (a JUMBF box in a JPEG's APP11 segment, a PNG's `caBX` chunk), the **IPTC / XMP** `DigitalSourceType` *trainedAlgorithmicMedia* and their `Credit` / `Creator tool`, the EXIF `Software` / `Artist`, a PNG's `tEXt` / `iTXt` chunks (prompt, parameters). First check what the exports already carry — they re-encode the grid, so probably nothing — then decide where it lives: a file of the explorer or a cell cleaned into a copy (*Save without metadata…*), a check on the imported images, a badge on the tiles showing an AI mark. An **invisible watermark** in the pixels (SynthID-like) is not metadata and stays out of reach. Sample images from Grok to be provided by the user. A case of the *Metadata viewer and editor* below — its **Remove AI marks** button | User request, 2026-11-04 | To discuss |
| **Metadata viewer and editor** — a file's metadata shown as **key / value** rows grouped by kind (EXIF, IPTC, XMP, PNG text, ICC, C2PA, a video's tags), opened by *Metadata…* in a tile's or a cell's context menu; a value **edited** in place, a row or a whole group **deleted** (✖), a **Remove AI marks** button (*Strip AI metadata* above). Reading: **MetadataExtractor** (.NET, Apache 2.0, NuGet), images and videos. Writing **never re-encodes** the image: **ExifTool** — reads, writes and deletes everything losslessly, videos and C2PA included — as a downloadable component of the Component updates window, like yt-dlp (recommended), or Windows' **WIC** metadata writer (EXIF / IPTC / XMP of JPEG, TIFF, PNG only, nothing to install). Written into the file itself after a confirmation, or into a copy, to be decided; out of the undo history | User request, 2026-11-04 | To design |
