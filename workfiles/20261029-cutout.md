# Cutout

> Working document — cutting out part of a cell's content automatically (background removal), as
> a cell effect.
> This file is the source of truth for the planned work until implemented,
> then the log of every adjustment made to it afterwards.

---

## Overview

The user wants to **cut out** (*détourer*) part of a content: keep a subject, make the rest of the
image transparent — **automatically**, no hand-drawn outline.

Agreed scope (Q&A #1–#3):

- **What is kept**: the **main subject**, found automatically when the effect is turned on; a
  **click** on another object of the image picks that one instead.
- **Where**: a new **cell effect**, *Cutout*, with its tab in the effects toolbar — on / off, kept
  settings, its own Reset, presets, undo, layers: everything § Effects of RULES.md says.
- **Contents**: **still images** first — photos, PDF pages, thumbnails, texts as rendered. Videos
  and animated GIFs keep the tab selectable, its checkbox **disabled with a tooltip saying why**
  (RULES.md § Effects Toolbar), until a later workfile.

Two offline ONNX models, never shipped, downloaded on demand like the other engines:

| Role | Model | Size | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main subject (automatic) | **BiRefNet** general, fp16 (`onnx-community/BiRefNet-ONNX`, `onnx/model_fp16.onnx`) | ~490 MB | MIT |
| Object under a click | **MobileSAM** (`Acly/MobileSAM`: image encoder + mask decoder) | ~45 MB | MIT |

Excluded: Bria RMBG 1.4 / 2.0 (non-commercial), Windows' `ImageObjectExtractor` (Copilot+ PCs only).

## Effect State

- `CutoutEffect`, an immutable record on `ImageLook` (`Cutout` / `KeptCutout`, a new
  `ImageEffect.Cutout`), modelled on `BlurEffect`: `Default`, `ToJson` / `FromJson`.
- Its settings:
  - **Mode**: `Subject` (the model's main subject) or `Picked` (the object under the clicked
    point(s)).
  - **Points**: the clicked points, each **included** or **excluded**, in **fractions of the image
    as loaded**, before any rotation or flip — like the Crop's sides (RULES.md § The Crop Exception), so the pick survives a turn, a
    flip, a crop, a resize and a layout change. Empty in `Subject` mode.
  - **Soften**: how far the edge fades, relative to the cell's size like the blur — 0 by default.
  - **Grow**: how far the edge is pushed out (positive) or eaten in (negative), relative to the
    cell's size — 0 by default.
  - **Invert**: keeps what the mask leaves out instead — off by default.
- **Default state**: off, mode `Subject`, no point, Soften 0, Grow 0, Invert off.
- The table of RULES.md § Scope and State holds unchanged: reset on a replaced image and on a shift
  after a deletion, kept on a swap and a layout change.
- Every `switch` over `ImageEffect` gets an explicit `Cutout` arm — several end in a `_ =>` that
  means Blur or Volume today (`ImageLook.TurnOff` / `Reset` / `Deactivate`, `LookPreset.Values`,
  `MainForm`'s icon switch).

## The Mask

- A **mask** is an 8-bit alpha plane at the size of the **bitmap as loaded** — uncropped,
  unoriented — so crop, orientation, fine angle and zoom map through it unchanged and the crop
  never recomputes it.
- It is **computed off the UI thread**, never in `Compositor.DrawCell`, which only reads it.
- **Cached on `SourceImage`**, next to `BandColor`, keyed by the mode and the points, swapped as a
  whole so an export thread reads it safely; counted in `SourceImage.MemoryBytes` (the undo
  history's budget). Kept: the last few keys, so an undo / redo between two picks is instant.
- **Not persisted**: an image loaded again computes its mask again.
- `ImageLook` holds only the settings — the history snapshots no pixels.

### Subject Mode

BiRefNet (general, fp16 export) on the whole bitmap resized to 1024 × 1024, ImageNet
normalisation, sigmoid on its output, the mask resized back to the bitmap's size. The fp16 export
is the user's choice for quality (Q&A #7); if ONNX Runtime's CPU kernels cannot run it, the run
takes the closest workable variant (the fp32 export) and reports it.

### Picked Mode

MobileSAM: the image encoder runs once per image (its embedding cached on `SourceImage` while the
Cutout tab is used), the mask decoder once per click with **every point** of the effect — included
ones labelled foreground, excluded ones background — a few milliseconds, so a pick feels live.

### Edge Options

Applied to the mask at draw size, not to the model's output: **Grow** dilates (positive) or erodes
(negative) the mask, then **Soften** blurs its edge, then **Invert** flips it — all three relative
to the cell's size, so the preview and an export at another size look the same (RULES.md §
Rendering). The cut-out bitmap is cached per mask and edge settings.

### While a Mask Is Computed

- The cell is drawn **as if the effect were off** until its mask is ready, then repainted
  (`GridPreview.RedrawCell`, through `BeginInvoke`).
- A **Copy or an export waits** for the masks it needs before `GridExport.Job.Capture`, under its
  usual lock and progress.
- (Indicator while computing: Open Question.)

## Rendering

- Applied in `Compositor.DrawCell`, inside its `DrawImage` closure: the bitmap drawn is the
  **bitmap × mask** (a cut-out bitmap with alpha, made once per mask and cached with it), so every
  later step — colors (alpha kept by the color matrix), blur, the preview, every export, a layer —
  shows it from one place.
- The transparent part shows what is behind the image: the Background's flat fill when it is on,
  the checkerboard / a PNG's alpha / white when it is off (RULES.md § The Background Exception).
- **The Background's extending fills** read the image as drawn in the cell (RULES.md § The
  Background's Edge Extension) — so they would extend the cut-out (Open Question).
- Position in the chain: after the orientation and the crop (the mask is mapped through them),
  before the fitting rule, zoom, fine angle, background, colors and blur.

## UI

### Tab

- **Cutout** tab in the effects toolbar, its icon in `EffectIcons`. Checked → mode `Subject`, its
  mask computed.
- Disabled checkbox, with its tooltip, on a video or an animated GIF: *Cutout works on still images
  only for now.*

### Options

Option thumbnails (RULES.md § Options Toolbar, `ThumbnailStrip`, 56 × 40):

| Thumbnail | Does |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | Mode `Subject`, the points cleared |
| **Pick** | Arms the click: the next click on the selected cell picks the object under it (mode `Picked`) |

Then **Soften** and **Grow** sliders, the **Invert** checkbox, then the effect's **Reset**. Acting on any option turns the
effect on (RULES.md § Options Toolbar).

### Click to Pick

- **Pick** armed: the cursor over the selected cell becomes a crosshair; a left click picks; Escape,
  a right click or a click elsewhere cancels. A flag of `GridPreview` checked early in
  `OnMouseDown` / `OnMouseMove`, and part of `InGesture` (one undo step per pick, none for a
  cancel) — lighter than the eyedropper's application-wide filter.
- The click is converted to the image as loaded through the fit math (`FitCalculator`, the crop,
  the orientation), stored in the effect's points.
- **Several points** (Q&A #6): a plain click starts a **new pick** — its point alone, included;
  **Shift + click** adds an included point; **Ctrl + click** adds an excluded one. Pick stays armed
  while Shift or Ctrl is held, so several points are placed in a row; a plain click disarms it after
  picking. Each click is one undo step.
- The points are drawn while the Cutout tab is selected and the effect on — a green dot for an
  included point, a green ring with a cross for an excluded one (RULES.md § On-Cell Helper
  Indicators), preview only.

## Engine

- `CutoutEngineFiles` (`Explorer/`), a copy of `VisionEngineFiles`: folder **`cutout-engine\`**
  next to the exe (kebab-case, ../CLAUDE.md § Folder Names), `EngineFile` records pinned to a
  Hugging Face revision with their SHA-256, `IsAvailable`, `DownloadAsync` through
  `DirectEngineDownload` (the ONNX Runtime fetched into `ocr-engine\` when missing; `runtimeShare`
  recomputed for this size).
- The sessions resolve the native DLLs first (`OcrEngineFiles.ResolveNativeLibraries`), use
  `IntraOpNumThreads = 2`, and are **released after 5 minutes idle**, like `TextEncoder`.
- **Download offer**: checking the Cutout checkbox without the engine opens the confirmation —
  what, how many MB, which folder, the runtime too when missing — like a global effect opening its
  file picker (RULES.md § Global Toolbar): **No** leaves the effect off; nothing is downloaded
  without that **Yes**. The ⚙ menu gets *Download cutout engine…* while it is missing. **Both
  models in one download** (~535 MB, Q&A #8): Pick works without another question.
- At implementation: pin the revisions, compute the SHA-256 from the pinned files, and read each
  model's input / output names and opset with ONNX Runtime before coding against them.

## Presets and History

- **Presets**: `LookPreset` gets a `cutout` key (`CutoutEffect.ToJson` / `FromJson`, named keys
  written by hand, a missing value giving its default) and its `Describe` line (`Cutout: subject` /
  `Cutout: picked, 3 points`, then Soften / Grow / Invert when not default). Restored after the crop and the orientation, like the others; the mask is
  computed on arrival.
- **Undo history**: covered by itself — the settings are on `ImageLook`. A restore of a step whose
  mask is not cached recomputes it, the cell drawn uncut meanwhile.

## Design Iterations

### Iteration 1 — 2026-10-29

Scope settled by the first batch (Q&A #1–#4). Exploration in two passes (subject judged tricky):
four scouts — the cell effect's plumbing, the engines' download, an async per-image computation
and an armed click, the available models. Initial design: a *Cutout* cell effect holding its mode
and points on `ImageLook`, its mask computed off the UI thread on the bitmap as loaded and cached
on `SourceImage`, applied in `Compositor.DrawCell`'s `DrawImage`; BiRefNet_lite for the subject,
MobileSAM for a click, in `cutout-engine\`, downloaded on demand.

### Iteration 2 — 2026-10-29

Second batch (Q&A #5–#8): the edge options — Soften, Grow, Invert — applied to the mask at draw
size; several points per pick, Shift adding and Ctrl excluding, drawn as helper indicators; the
subject model becomes **BiRefNet full fp16** (490 MB) instead of BiRefNet_lite, the fp32 export as
fallback if the CPU cannot run fp16; both models in one download (~535 MB).
