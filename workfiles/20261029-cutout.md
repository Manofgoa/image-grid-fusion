# Cutout

> Working document — cutting out part of a cell's content automatically (background removal), as
> a cell effect.
> This file is the source of truth for the planned work until implemented,
> then the log of every adjustment made to it afterwards.

---

## Overview

The user wants to **cut out** (*détourer*) part of a content: keep a subject, make the rest of the
image transparent — **automatically**, no hand-drawn outline.

Agreed scope (Q&A #1–#3):

- **What is kept**: the **main subject**, found automatically when the effect is turned on; a
  **click** on another object of the image picks that one instead.
- **Where**: a new **cell effect**, *Cutout*, with its tab in the effects toolbar — on / off, kept
  settings, its own Reset, presets, undo, layers: everything § Effects of RULES.md says.
- **Contents**: **still images** first — photos, single PDF pages, thumbnails, texts as rendered:
  whatever does not play (`SourceImage.CutsOut`). Videos, animated GIFs and contents of several pages
  keep the tab selectable, its checkbox **disabled with a tooltip saying why** (RULES.md § Effects
  Toolbar), until a later workfile.

Two offline ONNX models, never shipped, downloaded on demand like the other engines:

| Role | Model | Size | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main subject (automatic) | **IS-Net general use** (`isnet-general-use.onnx`, rembg's release v0.0.0) | ~179 MB | Apache 2.0 (DIS code; the weights carry no licence of their own) |
| Object under a click | **MobileSAM** (`Acly/MobileSAM`: image encoder + both mask decoders) | ~61 MB | MIT |

BiRefNet, the model chosen first (Q&A #7), does not run on a desktop's CPU: see Iteration 5.

Excluded: Bria RMBG 1.4 / 2.0 (non-commercial), Windows' `ImageObjectExtractor` (Copilot+ PCs only).

## Effect State

- `CutoutEffect`, an immutable record on `ImageLook` (`Cutout` / `KeptCutout`, a new
  `ImageEffect.Cutout`), modelled on `BlurEffect`: `Default`, `ToJson` / `FromJson`.
- Its settings:
  - **Mode**: `Subject` (the model's main subject) or `Picked` (the object under the clicked
    point(s)).
  - **Points**: the clicked points, each **included** or **excluded**, in **fractions of the image
    as loaded**, before any rotation or flip — like the Crop's sides (RULES.md § The Crop Exception), so the pick survives a turn, a
    flip, a crop, a resize and a layout change. Empty in `Subject` mode.
  - **Soften**: how far the edge fades, 0 to 1 for 0 to 2 % of the image's longer side
    (`CutoutEffect.MaxEdge`) — 0 by default.
  - **Grow**: how far the edge is pushed out (positive) or eaten in (negative), -1 to 1 for ±2 % of
    the image's longer side — 0 by default.
  - **Invert**: keeps what the mask leaves out instead — off by default.
  - **Background**: what becomes of the part the mask leaves out (§ The Background Kept) — one
    treatment, `Transparent` by default, its **amount** (0.5) and its **color** (black).
- **Default state**: off, mode `Subject`, no point, Soften 0, Grow 0, Invert off, background
  transparent.
- The table of RULES.md § Scope and State holds unchanged: reset on a replaced image and on a shift
  after a deletion, kept on a swap and a layout change.
- Every `switch` over `ImageEffect` gets an explicit `Cutout` arm — several end in a `_ =>` that
  means Blur or Volume today (`ImageLook.TurnOff` / `Reset` / `Deactivate`, `LookPreset.Values`,
  `MainForm`'s icon switch).

## The Mask

- A **mask** is an 8-bit alpha plane at the size of the **bitmap as loaded** — uncropped,
  unoriented — so crop, orientation, fine angle and zoom map through it unchanged and the crop
  never recomputes it.
- A mask is the model's output at its own resolution — 1024 × 1024 for the subject, the image read
  at 1024 on its longer side for a pick (`CutoutMask`) — stretched over the bitmap when applied.
- It is **computed off the UI thread** by `UI/CutoutService`, one job at a time, never in
  `Compositor.DrawCell`.
- **Cached on `SourceImage`**, next to `BandColor`: the last 6 masks, keyed by the page shown and
  `CutoutEffect.MaskKey` (the mode and the points), so an undo / redo between two picks or an edge
  option moved never runs the model again; the **cut-out** bitmap for the current settings
  (`SetCut`); MobileSAM's embedding of the page (`PickEmbedding`). All counted in
  `SourceImage.MemoryBytes` (the undo history's budget), set on the UI thread only.
- **Not persisted**: an image loaded again computes its mask again.
- `ImageLook` holds only the settings — the history snapshots no pixels.

### Subject Mode

IS-Net on the whole bitmap squashed to 1024 × 1024 over white, normalised as rembg does (each
channel over the image's brightest value, minus 0.5), its output stretched from its lowest value to
its highest. About 2 s on the CPU, 0.7 GB at its peak.

### Picked Mode

MobileSAM: the image encoder reads the image once — its longer side at 1024, about 1 s — its
embedding kept on `SourceImage`; the mask decoder runs once per click with **every point** of the
effect — included ones labelled foreground, excluded ones background, a padding point closing the
prompt — in a fraction of a second. One point: the **multi-mask** decoder, its best mask by
predicted IoU; several: the **single-mask** decoder, as Segment Anything advises.

### Edge Options

Applied when the cut-out is built, to the mask stretched to the bitmap's size (`CutoutMask.Apply`):
**Grow** takes the largest (positive) or smallest (negative) value within its radius — a square, in
linear time whatever the radius — then **Soften** blurs the edge with three box blurs, then
**Invert** flips it. Grow and Soften are shares of the image's **longer side**, so a bitmap
rendered at another size looks the same; the cut-out is built once per mask and edge settings.

### The Background Kept

The part the mask leaves out is kept, treated, instead of made transparent (Iteration 7, Q&A #15) —
**one treatment at a time**, a thumbnail strip (`CutoutBackgroundStrip`) followed by its slider:

| Thumbnail | The part left out | Slider |
|---|---|---|
| **Transparent** (default) | Transparent, as before | Disabled |
| **Opacity** | The image itself, see-through | Its opacity, 0–100 % |
| **Color** | The image under a solid color — a mask — the color option's split button (eyedropper, Color…, Sample: RULES.md § Color Options) | The color's opacity, 0–100 % |
| **Blur** | The image blurred | Its strength: a gaussian up to 3 % of the image's longer side |
| **Pixelate** | The image in squares | Their side: up to 8 % of the image's longer side |

- Built with the cut-out (`CutoutMask.Apply`): the treated image under, the kept part over it, mixed
  by the mask — so Grow, Soften and Invert shape the seam between them. Shares of the longer side, as
  the edge options: a bitmap rendered at another size looks the same.
- The treatment, its amount and its color are kept per strip, the slider showing the treatment
  pressed; Opacity, Color, Blur and Pixelate are options like the others — acting on one turns the
  effect on.

### Pick View

While the **Cutout tab is selected, the effect on and Pick pressed** — armed, or the mode `Picked`
(Q&A #12) — the selected cell shows the **whole image**: the image uncut drawn see-through (35 %) over
the cell as composed, so the part left out shows faintly and an object it holds can be clicked; the
kept part looks as it does. Preview only, like the crop's edit view: the other cells, the other tabs
and every export show the cutout.

### Hover Outline

While Pick is **armed**, the object a click would pick under the cursor is **outlined** as the mouse
moves (Q&A #13, #14) — a fluorescent green line over a dark halo (RULES.md § On-Cell Helper
Indicators), the image unchanged under it:

- The point under the cursor goes to MobileSAM's decoder with the pick's points as the click would —
  alone, or added to them with Shift (included) or Ctrl (left out) — the mask at half the size read
  (`CutoutEngine.Pick`'s scale), so a decode takes a fraction of a second.
- **One decode at a time**: a move while one runs only records the last point, decoded once it ends;
  the image's embedding is read as soon as Pick is armed (`CutoutService.EmbedAsync`).
- The outline is drawn through the cell's own mapping — an edge bitmap at the mask's size drawn by
  `Compositor.DrawCell` with the image's look — so crop, turn, flip, zoom and fine angle place it.
- Cleared when the cursor leaves the cell, when Pick is disarmed, or the look changes.

### While a Mask Is Computed

- The cell is drawn **as if the effect were off** until its cut-out is ready, then redrawn
  (`CutoutService.Completed` → `GridPreview.RedrawCell`). Every paint asks the service for every image
  still drawn uncut (`GridPreview.PaintCutout`); a request whose look changed before its turn is
  dropped, so dragging a slider computes the last value only.
- A **Copy or an export waits** for the cut-outs it draws before `GridExport.Job.Capture`
  (`MainForm.WaitForCutoutsAsync`, under `BeginExport`: the grid locked, *Cutting out the images…* in
  the status line). One that fails is drawn uncut; the failure is remembered for that page and mask,
  not retried, the status line saying why.
- Meanwhile the cell shows a **✂ Cutting out…** badge, its dots moving (Q&A #10) — a dark pill at the
  bottom of the cell, drawn by `GridPreview.OnPaint` only, never in an export; *✂ Waiting for the
  cutout engine* while the engine is missing. On the cells; a layer is redrawn when ready, without a
  badge.

## Rendering

- Drawn through **`SourceImage.DrawnBitmap`**: the cut-out — the bitmap × the mask, same size —
  while the Cutout is on and its cut-out matches the look, the page and the bitmap's size, else the
  bitmap. Every `Frame` is built from it (`Compositor.Draw` / `Render`, `GridPreview.FrameOf`,
  `GridLayers.FrameAt`, `GridExport.Job`), so `Compositor.DrawCell` draws it as the image itself and
  every later step — colors (alpha kept by the color matrix), blur, the preview, every export, a
  layer — shows it from one place (RULES.md § The Cutout Exception).
- The transparent part shows what is behind the image: the Background's flat fill when it is on,
  the checkerboard / a PNG's alpha / white when it is off (RULES.md § The Background Exception).
- **The Background's extending fills** read the image as drawn in the cell (RULES.md § The
  Background's Edge Extension), unchanged (Q&A #9): they extend the **cut-out** — its transparent
  edges leaving the bands mostly to the flat fill. No exception to the rule.
- Position in the chain: after the orientation and the crop (the mask is mapped through them),
  before the fitting rule, zoom, fine angle, background, colors and blur.

## UI

### Tab

- **Cutout** tab in the effects toolbar, its icon in `EffectIcons`. Checked → mode `Subject`, its
  mask computed.
- Disabled checkbox, with its tooltip, on a content that plays: *Cutout works on still images only
  for now: not on videos, animated GIFs nor content of several pages*.

### Options

Option thumbnails (RULES.md § Options Toolbar, `ThumbnailStrip`, 56 × 40):

| Thumbnail | Does |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | Mode `Subject`, the points cleared, Pick disarmed |
| **Pick** | Turns the effect on, then arms the click: the next click on the selected cell picks the object under it (mode `Picked`). Pressed while armed or while the mode is `Picked` (`CutoutModeStrip`) |

Then the **Grow** and **Soften** sliders with their labels (`Grow: +0.8 %`), the **Invert**
checkbox, then the effect's **Reset**. Acting on any option turns the effect on (RULES.md § Options
Toolbar).

### Click to Pick

- **Pick** armed: the cursor over the selected cell becomes a crosshair; a left click picks; Escape,
  a right click or a click elsewhere cancels, and so do another selection, another tab, the effect
  turned off. A flag of `GridPreview` checked first in `OnMouseDown` / `OnMouseMove`
  (`GridPreview.Cutout.cs`) — lighter than the eyedropper's application-wide filter — and **not** part
  of `InGesture`: each click is its own undo step, a cancel none.
- On a layer, Pick works in the layer's edit view, where it is the cell; a layer selected on the grid
  does not arm it.
- The click is converted to the image as loaded through the fit math (`FitCalculator`, the crop,
  the orientation), stored in the effect's points.
- **Several points** (Q&A #6): a plain click starts a **new pick** — its point alone, included;
  **Shift + click** adds an included point; **Ctrl + click** adds an excluded one. Pick stays armed
  while Shift or Ctrl is held, so several points are placed in a row; a plain click disarms it after
  picking. Each click is one undo step.
- The points are drawn while the Cutout tab is selected and the effect on — a green dot for an
  included point, a green ring with a cross for an excluded one (RULES.md § On-Cell Helper
  Indicators), preview only.

## Engine

- `CutoutEngineFiles` (`Imaging/`), a copy of `VisionEngineFiles`: folder **`cutout-engine\`**
  next to the exe (kebab-case, ../CLAUDE.md § Folder Names), four `EngineFile` records with their
  SHA-256 — IS-Net from rembg's GitHub release, MobileSAM's encoder and two decoders pinned to a
  Hugging Face revision — `IsAvailable`, `DownloadAsync` through `DirectEngineDownload` (the ONNX
  Runtime fetched into `ocr-engine\` when missing).
- `CutoutEngine` (`Imaging/`) loads each model the first time it is needed: the native DLLs resolved
  first (`OcrEngineFiles.ResolveNativeLibraries`), `IntraOpNumThreads = 2`, **without the memory
  arena** so what a run takes is given back; **released after 5 minutes unused** by `CutoutService`,
  like `TextEncoder`.
- **Download offer**: turning the Cutout on without the engine — its checkbox or any of its options —
  opens the confirmation —
  what, how many MB, which folder, the runtime too when missing — like a global effect opening its
  file picker (RULES.md § Global Toolbar): **No** leaves the effect off; nothing is downloaded
  without that **Yes**; on Yes the effect turns on, its cells badged *Waiting for the cutout engine*
  until the download ends, its progress in the status line. The ⚙ menu gets *Download the cutout
  engine…*, after the yt-dlp companions, while it is missing. **Both models in one download**
  (~240 MB, Q&A #8): Pick works without another question.

## Presets and History

- **Presets**: `LookPreset` gets a `cutout` key (`CutoutEffect.ToJson` / `FromJson`, named keys
  written by hand, a missing value giving its default) and its `Describe` line (`Cutout: subject` /
  `Cutout: picked, 3 points`, then grow / soften / inverted when not default). Restored right after
  the crop, the orientation before it; the mask is computed on arrival.
- **Undo history**: covered by itself — the settings are on `ImageLook`, `ImageLook.ChangedEffects`
  names the Cutout. A restore whose mask is kept is cut out again from it; one whose mask is not runs
  the model, the cell drawn uncut meanwhile.

## Design Iterations

### Iteration 1 — 2026-10-29

Scope settled by the first batch (Q&A #1–#4). Exploration in two passes (subject judged tricky):
four scouts — the cell effect's plumbing, the engines' download, an async per-image computation
and an armed click, the available models. Initial design: a *Cutout* cell effect holding its mode
and points on `ImageLook`, its mask computed off the UI thread on the bitmap as loaded and cached
on `SourceImage`, applied in `Compositor.DrawCell`'s `DrawImage`; BiRefNet_lite for the subject,
MobileSAM for a click, in `cutout-engine\`, downloaded on demand.

### Iteration 2 — 2026-10-29

Second batch (Q&A #5–#8): the edge options — Soften, Grow, Invert — applied to the mask at draw
size; several points per pick, Shift adding and Ctrl excluding, drawn as helper indicators; the
subject model becomes **BiRefNet full fp16** (490 MB) instead of BiRefNet_lite, the fp32 export as
fallback if the CPU cannot run fp16; both models in one download (~535 MB).

### Iteration 3 — 2026-10-29

Third batch (Q&A #9–#11): the extending fills keep the existing rule and extend the cut-out; a
*Cutting out…* indicator shows on the cell while its mask is computed; no unit tests. No open
question left.

### Iteration 4 — 2026-10-29 — ✅ Implemented

Go given: code, unit tests (none, Q&A #11) and documentation, in a worktree on `feature/cutout`.

### Iteration 5 — 2026-10-29 — 🧭 Implementation choices

Choices the run took on its own, measured on this machine (31 GB of memory, 5.6 GB of commit left
while the run went):

- **The subject model is IS-Net general use, not BiRefNet** — the closest variant that works. Every
  BiRefNet export was measured through ONNX Runtime on the CPU, 2 threads: **full fp16** loads in 7 s
  then runs for more than 10 minutes without ending one image (its fp16 kernels); **full fp32** fails
  to allocate in its deformable convolutions (`aspp_deforms`), whatever the arena; **lite fp32** runs
  12 s an image at **6.3 GB** at its peak, and fails as soon as less memory is left — which this
  machine already was. **IS-Net**: 1.7 s, 0.7 GB, cutouts clean on the samples tried (a tiger, a car).
  Its licence: the DIS code is Apache 2.0, the weights carry no licence of their own; downloaded from
  rembg's GitHub release `v0.0.0`, pinned by its SHA-256. The download drops from ~535 to ~240 MB.
- **MobileSAM's two decoders**: the multi-mask one for a single point (its best mask by predicted
  IoU), the single-mask one for several points, both in the one download.
- **The cut-out is drawn through `SourceImage.DrawnBitmap`**, every `Frame` built from it, rather than
  inside `DrawCell`'s `DrawImage` closure: `DrawCell` stays untouched and draws it as the image;
  RULES.md § The Cutout Exception says so, a new reader taking `DrawnBitmap`.
- **Grow and Soften are shares of the image's longer side**, applied when the cut-out is built at the
  bitmap's size, not of the cell's at draw size: the cut-out is built once per settings rather than
  per draw size, and still looks the same in the preview and an export. ±2 % / 2 % at the slider's
  ends (`CutoutEffect.MaxEdge`).
- **Pick is not part of `InGesture`**: held while armed, it would have merged a Shift / Ctrl series
  into one undo step; each click is its own step, as the UI section asks.
- **"Still images" is `SourceImage.CutsOut`, whatever does not play**: a PDF or a text of several
  pages plays its pages, so its checkbox is disabled too, not only videos and GIFs.
- **The badge** shows on the cells only; a layer is redrawn once its cut-out is ready, without one.
  *✂ Waiting for the cutout engine* while it downloads.
- **Pick on a layer** works in its edit view; a layer selected on the grid does not arm it.
- **A failed cutout is not retried** for the same page and mask; the status line says why, the image
  drawn uncut. ONNX Runtime's, memory, file and native-library errors are caught.
- **Masks kept**: the last 6 per image, MobileSAM's embedding of the page shown, all in
  `SourceImage.MemoryBytes`.
- **The ⚙ menu's** *Download the cutout engine…* sits after the yt-dlp companions, not in a group of
  its own: a group with one item would leave two separators side by side once it is downloaded.
- **The download offer** comes on every route turning the Cutout on — its checkbox, Subject, Pick, a
  slider, Invert — not the checkbox alone.

Checks done: the build, without a warning; the engine through a scratch harness compiling
`CutoutEngine`, `CutoutMask` and `CutoutEffect` — the subject of two photos, a pick of one and of three
points, Grow / Soften / Invert, the preset JSON round trip; in the app, the tab and its options, the
download offer, and a **No** leaving the effect off. Not checked in the app: a cut-out drawn in a cell,
Pick on a cell, undo / redo, an export, a layer, a preset restored, the download itself — driving the
app moved the real mouse, so it stopped there; the user's test covers them.

### Iteration 6 — 2026-10-29 — ⚙️ Post-implementation — Pick shows the whole image, live preview

User's request, after testing:

- In Pick mode, show the **whole image** again — the part cut away drawn see-through — so an object
  the cutout left out can be clicked.
- In Pick mode, follow the mouse and show, as close to live as possible, **what would be cut out**
  under the cursor — or outline it, if that costs less.

Design questions asked before the code: Q&A #12–#14.

### Iteration 7 — 2026-10-29 — ⚙️ Post-implementation — The background kept, with an effect

User's request, right after: an option to **keep the background** — the part the cutout leaves out —
instead of making it transparent, with an effect applied to it: **opacity**, **mask**, **blur** or
**pixelation**.

Design question asked before the code: Q&A #15.

Q&A #12–#15 answered: the Pick view while the tab is selected in Pick mode; an **outline** of the
object under the hovering cursor; one background treatment at a time with its amount. Domain sections
